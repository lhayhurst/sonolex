# Warnings and Important Notes

Before connecting this unit to other devices, turn off the power to all units to prevent malfunctions and/or damage to speakers or other devices. If you need to connect/disconnect wires during use, always connect the cable to the OP–XY first.

Never connect the 3.5 mm plugs going from the OP–XY's inputs or outputs to a microphone input with active phantom power — this could damage the OP–XY.

Charge the unit using only 5V USB power (computer USB port or dedicated USB charger).

Ambient working temperature: 10°–35°C (50°–95°F)
Ambient storage temperature: 0°–35°C (32°–86°F)

To keep the battery healthy, charge at least every 6 months. If not used for a long time, it may not charge again.

Clean the shell with a lightly damp cloth. Let dry before usage.

Do not try to charge or use a unit with a seemingly damaged battery. Only a battery supplied by teenage engineering installed by qualified personnel should be used. Disposal of a battery into fire or a hot oven, or mechanically crushing or cutting of a battery, can result in an explosion.

To prevent possible hearing damage, do not listen at high sound levels for long periods. Store small parts out of the reach of children and infants.

# Hardware Overview

OP–XY is a precision tool, made in black anodized aluminium, with encoders in a fading gray scale from black to white. The backlit low profile keyboard is tactile, durable and responsive. The display is a custom made color LCD, mounted directly onto the keyboard. Soft velcro rings on the bottom allow the unit to be attached to a surface, case or stand.

## Power On and Charging

Flip the power switch on the right side to the up position to power on. The display shows the logo and firmware version, then arrives at the last selected track. Flip down to power off. Data is stored automatically.

Charged through the USB-C port on the right side. Connect to a computer or USB charger and keep connected until the battery is fully charged (indicated by level meter). Check battery in system settings. Charge at least every 6 months.

# Main Modes

OP–XY features four main modes:

Instrument: 8 track buttons control and edit 8 instrument tracks (drums, synths, samplers).

Auxiliary: 8 track buttons for intelligent transposition, send effects, external inputs/outputs, and punch-in FX™.

Arrange: Build songs by chaining scenes made up of patterns from 8 tracks.

Mix: Control levels, panning, EQ, compression and FX sends for each track.

# Modules

Each main mode (except arrange) features four sub-modes called modules, edited via four encoders. Switch between modules using the four buttons underneath the screen (M1–M4). Some screens have extra parameters accessed by holding shift.

# Track Buttons

Tracks are divided into instruments and auxiliary. Select a track by pressing one of the 8 track buttons under the encoders.

Active track lights up white (instrument) or red (auxiliary). Numbered labels represent 8 instrument tracks; icons represent 8 auxiliary tracks.

Hold shift + track button to select a preset for that track.

Linking tracks: Hold one track button and press tracks to link (up to 4). The held track controls all linked tracks. Linked tracks can still be played separately from the primary track.

# Sequencer

The step sequencer runs across the length of the device and is the heart of the OP–XY.

## Step Sequencing

Select note: Press a note on the keyboard. OP–XY remembers the last played note.
Sequence note: Press a step — it lights up confirming recording.
Check note: Hold a step to see which notes are recorded.
Sequence chords: Hold notes/chord then press a step, or hold a step and play notes.
Edit step: Hold step, press a note to add/remove.
Extend note: Hold a step and press another step to set length. Press again to toggle full step or overlap.
Nudge step: Hold step then press −/+ for fine adjustments (only when quantisation not set to 100).
Sequence one sound only: Hold a sound on keyboard then tap record to view only that sound's steps.
Parameter lock: Hold a step then rotate a knob to lock that parameter value to the step (all four modules, but not players).
Clear: Hold record + stop until red LEDs fill the sequencer.
Undo: Press shift + record (one undo stage).
Copy step: Hold a step, then press an empty step to paste.
Change sequence octave/semitone: Hold shift + −/+ to change octave (synth/sampler) or semitone (drum sampler).

## Live Recording

Hold record + press play to arm. Step 1 flashes red. Begin playing on keyboard — sequencer starts and records. Steps turn red (recording) then white (recorded).

While song plays, hold record to live record notes. Hold record + rotate a knob to record parameter automation.

To lock recording mode while playing: hold record + play.

Count-in: Hold record, press play, then press play again.

## Step Recording

While paused, hold record. Step 1 lights red. Keep holding record and play keyboard — notes fill sequencer sequentially.

Skip step: While holding record, press +. Go back: press −.
Remove a step: tap it.
Clear track: Hold record + stop until red LEDs fill sequencer.

## Bar (Extending Sequences)

Bar controls sequence length, quantisation, note length, grooves and automation smoothing.

Track scale: Hold bar + accidental key. Determines time per step (per track). Available scales: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 16, 1/2.

Add/remove bars: Hold bar + press +/−. Maximum 4 bars. Combined with track scale: max 64 bars or 1024 1/16th notes.

Duplicate bar: Hold bar + shift, press +.

Sequence length: Hold bar + press step sequencer to set number of steps.

Switch bar: Press/tap bar.

Encoders while holding bar:
- Dark gray: Track quantization (100 = no nudging)
- Mid gray: Step-sequenced note length
- Light gray: Groove (overrides swing in tempo; select style in tempo screen)
- White: Smoothing between parameter locks

Clear notes: Hold bar + M1
Clear params: Hold bar + M2
Clear all: Hold bar + M4

Lock bar on screen: Shift + bar (toggle)

# Step Components

Step components are variations applied to specific steps that alter randomizing pitch, bending, skipping, multiplying steps, and more.

To add: Make a sequence → Hold shift → Select steps on sequencer → Press a natural key (step component) → Use accidental keys to set value → Release shift.

To remove: Hold shift, select steps, press the natural key of the applied component.

Each step can have any combination of all 14 step components.

## Step Component Reference Table

Pulse: Repeat step 1–9 times, or random steps.
Hold: Hold step 1–9 times, or random steps.
Multiply: Divide into 1–8 trigs (ratchet effect), or random trigs. Key 9 = 3 trigs.
Velocity: Force to 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 100, 112, 127, 0, or random.
Ramp Up: 2–6 steps across 1 octave; 2–6 steps across 3 octaves.
Ramp Down: 2–6 steps across 1 octave; 2–6 steps across 3 octaves.
Random: 2–6 steps across 1 or 3 octaves.
Portamento: Amount 10%–90%, or random amount.
Bend: Shapes — down/up, up/down, bump down, bump up, spring out, spring in, fade down, fade up, random 1, random 2.
Tonality: Ignore chord progression, transpose only, octave up, fifth up, third up, chromatic up, chromatic down, quantize 33%, quantize 66%, quantize 100%.
Jump: Jump to step 1/5/9/13, one step forward, one step back, forward or back, stay on step, align track position, random step.
Skip Parameter Lock: Play every param lock, every 2nd–9th, random.
Skip Step Component: Play every component, every 2nd–9th, random.
Skip Trigger: Play every trig, every 2nd–9th, random.

# Players

Players create variations on existing sequences such as arpeggios and chord triggers. Add a player: press player to open, press again to enable. Hold shift + player to change style.

## Arpeggio

Dark gray: Speed
Mid gray: Pattern (up, down, up/down, up/repeat/down, random, play order)
Light gray: Range
White: Hold (on/off)

Shift + dark gray: Note length
Shift + mid gray: Style (order of note playback)
Shift + light gray: Glide
Shift + white: Stereo (pan per note)

## Maestro

Hold shift + play notes to record to maestro. Maestro transposes recorded notes when you press a key — great for chord playback.

Dark gray: Roll (strum amount)
Mid gray: Pattern (up, down, up/down, random)
White: Hold (on/off)

## Hold

Hold will sustain whatever notes you play until the next note. Great for basses and chords. Press stop or disable player to turn off.

# Project

OP–XY can store thousands of projects. Each project has a versions folder for past versions.

New: Hold M1 to create (auto-saves if enabled).
Save: Press M2 (creates a version). Auto-save on by default.
Rename: Press M3. Use dark gray encoder to select character, mid gray to edit, module keys to confirm/navigate/cancel/delete.
Configure: Press M4 for project settings.
Save As: Shift + M2.

Projects folder (shift + project):
- M1: Load
- M2: History (all saves/auto-saves)
- M3: Duplicate
- Hold M4: Delete

## Project Settings

Dark gray: Select between general, tempo, voices, MIDI settings.
Mid gray: Choose setting to edit.
Light gray/white: Adjust value.

Tempo: Time signature (3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 12/8), groove type.
General: Transpose all notes including drums.
Voices: Assign voices to tracks (24 voices polyphony, auto-assigned by default).
MIDI: Set MIDI channel on each of 16 tracks.

Exit: Press M1.

# Tempo

Press tempo in any screen. Tap tempo to set BPM.

Dark gray: Adjust tempo.
Mid gray: Groove style.
Light gray: Clockwise past center = swing, counter-clockwise = shuffle.
White: Metronome volume (click to enable/disable).

## Grooves

Grooves change timing and velocity of sequenced notes.

Available groove types:
- Z Grooves: Slight randomisation to timing and velocity for human feel.
- Shuffle: Standard swing groove.
- Half Shuffle: Lighter version of shuffle.
- Bombora: Pushes beat against offbeats, breaking up twos and fours.
- Wobbly: Extreme groove for funky patterns.
- Gaussian: Mathematical groove pattern.
- Accents: Emphasises important beats.
- Island Nod: Head-nodding groove style.

# Workflow: Patterns, Scenes, Songs and Projects

Each track (instrument and auxiliary) holds up to 9 patterns. Each pattern holds max 120 notes.

Scenes: Arrangements of patterns across tracks. 99 available scenes. Scenes store pattern arrangement, mix settings, volumes and mutes. Scene length = longest pattern.

Songs: Arrangements of scenes. 9 songs per project. Max 96 scenes per song.

Projects: Contain patterns, scenes and songs. Thousands can be stored.

# Instrument Mode

8 instrument tracks. Each can be a sampler or built-in synth engine. Select tracks 1–8. Hold shift + M1 to change engine. Hold shift + track button to change preset.

## Engine (M1)

M1 is where you find the synth, drum or sampler. Hold shift + M1 to select engine. Encoders control engine-specific parameters (see Synth Engines section).

## Envelopes (M2)

Two envelopes per track: amplitude and filter. Click an encoder to switch between them.

Dark gray: Attack
Mid gray: Decay
Light gray: Sustain
White: Release

Shift + M2 (Play Mode):
- Dark gray: Poly / Mono / Legato
- Mid gray: Portamento amount
- Light gray: Bend range
- White: Preset volume

## Filter (M3)

M3 holds the filter. Hold shift + M3 to change filter type.

Dark gray: Cutoff frequency
Mid gray: Resonance
Light gray: Envelope amount (how much filter envelope moves filter)
White: Key tracking

Shift + M3 (Track Send):
- Dark gray: Aux out send
- Mid gray: Tape send
- Light gray: FX I send
- White: FX II send

## LFO (M4)

M4 holds the LFO. Hold shift + M4 to change LFO type.

Four LFO types: Element, Random, Tremolo, Value.

Common parameters:
- Dark gray: Source/Rate (anti-clockwise = tempo sync, clockwise = continuous)
- Mid gray: Amount
- Light gray: Destination (module to modulate; regular or 'free' — free doesn't reset on keypress)
- White: Parameter (encoder on destination module to modulate)

Duck: Sidechain pumping effect.
- Dark gray: Source (tracks 1–8 instrument, 9–16 auxiliary, or metronome). Click to switch between audio and note data source.
- Mid gray: Amount
- Light gray: Hold time
- White: Release

Element: Uses gyroscope, microphone, amp envelope, or sum of all three as modulation source.

Random: Random value generator. Shift + mid gray = envelope (fade in/out).

Tremolo:
- Dark gray: Speed
- Mid gray: Vibrato amount (pitch)
- Light gray: Volume modulation (tremolo)
- White: Envelope (fade in/out)
- Shift + mid gray: Shape

Value: Continuous or triggered LFO with standard destination/parameter routing.

## Preset Settings

Shift + instrument to enter. Dark gray selects between settings and modulation.

Settings tab: Tuning (microtonal adjustments, transposition, up to 11 user tunings where you set pitch per note), high pass filter, velocity sensitivity, portamento style, stereo width.

Mod tab: Route modwheel, aftertouch, pitchbend, and velocity to control various synth parameters.

User tuning: Open preset settings → settings → tuning. Light gray knob to select tuning slot. M4 to edit. Play a note on keyboard, use dark gray for cents adjustment, mid gray for micro-cents.

## Presets

Shift + track button (in instrument mode) to view/change preset.

Shift + dark gray: Switch between category and engine view.
Dark gray: Select category or engine.
Mid gray/light gray/white: Select preset.
Click encoder: Confirm selection.

Scramble: Hold track button + M1.
Copy preset: Hold track button + M2.
Paste preset: Hold destination track button + M3.
Save preset: Hold track button + M4.
Rename preset: Select user preset + M3.
Delete preset: Select user preset + M4.

# Auxiliary Mode

8 auxiliary tracks: Brain™ (track 1), Punch-in FX™ (track 2), External MIDI (track 3), External CV (track 4), External Audio (track 5), Tape (track 6), FX I (track 7), FX II (track 8).

## Brain™

Intelligent transposition. Brain listens and figures out the key signature of your song or selected tracks.

M1 (Brain):
- Dark gray: Manual/Auto key detection
- Mid gray: Key selection
- Light gray: Scale selection
- White: Link instrument tracks to brain (riff while transposing live)

M2 (Routing): Click encoders to switch between tracks 1–4 and 5–8. Rotate to add/remove tracks from brain routing. Only routed tracks are transposed and affect automatic key detection.

## Punch-in FX™

Auxiliary track 2. Use the musical keyboard to play, record and perform punch-in FX. Lower octave affects percussion tracks, higher octave affects melodic tracks. Some FX use gyroscope and pitchbend.

Per-track punch-in: In any instrument track, hold shift + press keyboard keys. Low octave = individual track, high octave = all tracks in group.

Punch-in FX can be sequenced and recorded like any other track.

## External MIDI

Auxiliary track 3. Control external MIDI devices via USB-C or multi-out jack.

M1: Channel (dark gray), Bank (mid gray), Program (light gray).
M2/M3: 8 MIDI CCs. Rotate encoders to edit values. Hold shift + rotate to select CC number.
M4: LFO with speed, amount, destination, parameter controls.

## External CV

Auxiliary track 4. CV output on tip (left channel), gate on ring (right channel). Set multi-out to CV/gate. Use musical keyboard to play and sequence notes on connected CV devices.

## External Audio

Auxiliary track 5. Input audio via 3.5mm jack. Send auxiliary audio via multi-out (set to audio).

M1:
- Dark gray: Input source (mic, headset, audio input, USB audio, main output)
- Mid gray: Drive/gain (analog inputs only)
- Light gray: Level in master mix
- White: Mix into main output

M2 (Routing): Click encoders for tracks 1–4 / 5–8. Rotate to route tracks through aux audio output.

M3 (Filter): Dark gray = high-pass cutoff, white = low-pass cutoff. Shift + mid gray = tape send, shift + light gray/white = FX send levels.

M4: LFO.

## Tape

Auxiliary track 6. Picks out and rearranges audio from routed tracks for glitchy effects.

M1:
- Dark gray: Pitch
- Mid gray: Speed
- Light gray: Loop length
- White: Mix (tape vs original)

M2 (Routing): Route tracks through tape.
M3 (Filter): High-pass/low-pass. Shift + light gray/white = FX send levels.
M4: LFO.

## FX I and FX II

Auxiliary tracks 7 and 8. Two FX send slots. Any sound-producing track can be sent to FX I and II. FX I can also be sent to FX II.

Hold shift + press FX I or FX II to change effect. Use encoders to select.

M1: FX engine parameters.
M2: Routing (click encoders for tracks 1–4 / 5–8).
M3: Filter (high-pass/low-pass). In FX I, shift + white = send level to FX II.
M4: LFO.

Create new patterns, move between them, and arrange into songs.

White knob: Navigate between existing patterns.

M1: New pattern (max 9 per track).

Shift + M1: Clone scene.
Shift + M2: Copy scene.

Sequence scenes into a song. Enter: Hold shift in arrange, press arrange.

M2: Play recorded sample.
M4: Delete recorded sample.

Dark gray: Input source.

Light gray: Gain.

Quick sample recording with pitch mapping.

Press any key to start sampling (selected key = tuning reference).

Editing:
- Dark gray: Sample start
- Mid gray: Loop start
- Light gray: Loop end
- White: Sample end
(Click knobs for finer adjustment)

Shift + dark gray: Playback direction (forward/backward)
Shift + mid gray: Tune
Shift + light gray: Loop crossfade
Shift + white: Sample gain

## Drum Sampler

24 one-shot samples across the keyboard.

Key select: Press key to select. Hold M1: Record to selected key. M2/M3: Previous/next sample. M4: Clear sample from key.

Editing:
- Dark gray: Tune
- Mid gray: Sample start
- Light gray: Sample end
- White: Play mode (key, oneshot, mute group, loop)

Shift + dark gray: Direction
Shift + mid gray: Pan
Shift + light gray: Sample fade
Shift + white: Sample gain

Copy: Hold key + M2. Paste: Hold key + M3.
Multi-select: Hold key + M4.

- Transient: Divides by loudest transients. Adjust slice start/end per key, set number of slices.

- Tap: Tap M1 to play and insert slice points. M2 to stop. Press shift + key to delete slice.

## Multisampler

Record complex multi-zone samples. Max 24 zones (~3 per octave). Fills down automatically (pitches samples down to fill gaps).

Key select, record, navigate, clear same as drum sampler.

Editing: Same as one shot synth sampler (sample start, loop start/end, sample end, direction, pan, fade, gain, loop type).

## Sample Folder

Stores all samples. Use to load any sample into any sampler.

OP–XY supports AIFF and WAV files. Load via MTP connection to computer. Delete samples from device via computer MTP connection.

Only folders created directly within the 'samples' folder will show on OP–XY (no nested subfolders). Filenames: alphanumeric characters, -, # and space only.

# Synth Engines

## Prism

Versatile engine for basses, synth leads and more.
- Dark gray: Shape

## Simple

Quick basic patches for leads and plucks.
- Dark gray: Shape

## Wavetable

Smoothly transition between predefined oscillator shapes. 9 wavetables included.

# FX Engines

6 built-in send FX assignable to two FX tracks. Change: Auxiliary mode → select FX I or FX II → shift + FX button → white knob to select → click or M1 to confirm.

## Chorus

Duplicates and shifts pitch/timing for wider sound.
- Dark gray: Rate
- Mid gray: Depth
- Light gray: Feedback (creates pitch-modulated delay at higher amounts)
- White: Stereo width

## Delay

Repeating echo effect.

# TE Boot

Access: Power off, then hold com while powering on.

Options:
1. Upload firmware (track button 1)
7. Factory reset (track button 7)
8. System menu / function test (track button 8)

Firmware update: Press track 1, connect USB, copy firmware file to removable disk, eject, wait for update.

Factory reset: Press track 7, follow instructions. All user content will be removed.

Function test: Press track 8. Buttons showing red indicate issues — contact support.

Reset volume pot calibration: Press track 8, then M2/track 2.

Exit te boot: Power off and on again.
