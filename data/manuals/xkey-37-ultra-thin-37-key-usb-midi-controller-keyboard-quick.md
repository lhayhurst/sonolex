# Introduction

Congratulations on your purchase of Xkey 37, a professional ultra-thin 37-key USB MIDI controller keyboard with polyphonic aftertouch for Mac, PC and mobile devices that provides everything you need to control software synthesizers, DAWs / sequencing software, notation software, other MIDI equipment and much more, wherever you go!

# Getting Started

To start using Xkey 37, connect it to your computer using the included USB cable. The USB-C port of Xkey 37 is located on the right under the keys. This is also a good time to connect the Xcable adapter for MIDI and pedal connection to the left side. No drivers are required (plug-and-play). This powers the keyboard and is used to transfer MIDI data to your computer. For the default and more common USB connector ("type A"), a cable is included. For "type C" a different cable or adapter is needed (not included). You will also need an adapter if you want to connect Xkey 37 to a phone or tablet. For instance, many Apple devices require the Apple Lightning to USB 3 Camera Connector while some Android devices require a so-called "USB OTG" adapter. Please double check the manual of your phone or tablet on how to connect USB accessories if you are unsure or contact technical support. Please note that this document covers the setup and the various built-in functions. It is not intended to be an introduction to MIDI. If you are new to MIDI, a good start is usually the manual of your DAW or notation or sequencing software. A good technical resource and starting point is www.midi.org and various online forums and user groups.

# Software

Since Xkey 37 is a MIDI controller that only sends MIDI data such as "Note On", "Note Off", "Pitch", "Velocity", etc., it cannot generate any sounds on its own. The sounds will be created by the software running on your Mac, PC or mobile device, usually so-called virtual instruments. Xkey 37 works with every common and major MIDI compatible software. Suggested software includes: Bitwig Studio 8-Track (Windows, macOS, Linux) — a powerful DAW supporting MIDI, virtual instruments, and professional recording; Cubasis LE from Steinberg or GarageBand from Apple (iOS). For Windows, macOS and iPad, the Xkey Plus editor software is strongly recommended as it allows changing velocity and aftertouch curves, checking Xkey status, and updating firmware. Available at http://en.esi.ms/123.

# Frequent Topics

Latency: A common issue, especially on Windows, is the delay between hitting a key and hearing sound. This latency is not caused by Xkey 37 but by your audio interface/soundcard and its driver. The best solution is to use a professional quality audio interface with low-latency drivers and to make sure the virtual instrument and DAW are correctly set up. No sound: Since Xkey 37 does not generate sound by itself, a virtual instrument, DAW with synthesizer plugin, or any other MIDI-compatible app that plays sounds is required.

# Main Functions

Xkey 37 has 37 full-sized, fully velocity-sensitive keys with polyphonic aftertouch, plus function buttons on the left side providing important controls.

## Octave Control

OCTAVE + and OCTAVE − buttons move the octave range played by the 37 keys up or down. Pressing minus shifts all sounds one octave lower; pressing plus shifts one octave higher. Pressing both buttons simultaneously resets the octave range to the default setting.

## Factory Reset

Hold both OCTAVE + and OCTAVE − while plugging in the USB cable to a computer to reset Xkey 37 to factory defaults.

## Modulation

The MODULATION button sends MIDI modulation controller data (CC 1). This button is pressure sensitive — the data sent depends on how strongly you push the button.

## Pitch Bend

PITCH BEND + and PITCH BEND − buttons pitch the sound up or down via MIDI pitch bend controller data. These buttons are pressure sensitive — the data sent depends on how strongly you push either button.

## Sustain

The SUSTAIN button enables or disables MIDI sustain functionality (CC 64). When pressed down, sustain mode is activated; when released, sustain mode is disabled.

## Xcable Connections

The Xcable connects to the left side of Xkey 37 and provides: a MIDI output with 5-pin DIN connector, and two 1/4" connectors for a sustain pedal and an expression pedal.

## Connectivity Summary

USB-C port (right side, under keys): power and MIDI data transfer. Xcable port (left side): MIDI DIN output, sustain pedal (1/4" jack), expression pedal (1/4" jack). Included cable: USB-C to USB-A. Not included: USB-C to USB-C cable, mobile device adapters (Lightning to USB 3 Camera Connector for Apple, USB OTG for Android).
