# Overview

Clean started as a question: Can clean be as fun as dirty? Distortion circuits are always bursting with character and interesting details that react to the way you play. Is it possible to enjoy the touch sensitivity of an overdrive, or the unwieldy response of a fuzz, on a clean instrument?

Clean is a "creative compressor" that brings the joyful and exploratory energy of distortion circuits to compression. Your playing dynamics are rewarded, creating a more interactive and lively version of your instrument that moves and shifts as you play. From tube-like sag, to drifting equalization, to softening swells.

Clean is entirely analog, and built from scratch using studio-grade parts. It's true stereo, utilizes two stages of compression, and features some unique refinements like emphasis filters and a dynamic EQ. Save an up-front enhancer to one preset for instrument work and an end-of-chain glue setting to another for mixes.

# What's Inside

Clean is an original, VCA-based circuit. It is not based on any existing compressors, but is instead a brand new exploration of how far compression can go.

Features:
- External Sidechain
- All-Analog Signal Path
- Two-Stage Compression
- True Stereo
- Adaptive Envelope Follower
- Dynamic EQ
- Emphasis Filtering (cassette-style noise reduction)
- Physical Modeling (organic fluctuations of a wobbling spring)
- Feedforward Blending
- Dry Blend
- Noise Gate

# Setup



## Power

Clean requires a 9V DC, center negative power supply with at least 300 mA of current.

## I/O

Clean can be used in mono, stereo, or mono to stereo. The default setting will automatically work for either mono or stereo:
- Mono in, mono out (TS cable)
- Stereo in, stereo out (TRS cable)

Many stereo devices use dual mono jacks, so you may need a TRS to dual TS-style cable.

If you have a mono input but want to split it to stereo output: Turn on the MISO dip switch. To create a unique stereo image: Turn on the SPREAD dip switch.

# Getting Started

The most important step is to get your SENSITIVITY right. Clean is a dynamic effect that reacts to the loudness of your playing. If SENSITIVITY is too high you will be overwhelmed, too low and you won't get much at all. Keep your eye on the left LED and make adjustments until you see it moving at a comfortable playing volume.

Suggested starting point:
- Turn up DYNAMICS and mess with PHYSICS to experience fluttering sag
- Get the EQ moving to introduce some vibe
- Hold down the AUX switch to turn it all into a swell

You should hear clear, balanced compression. Clean gives you many ways to smoosh, shift, and shape your sound.

# Controls



## SENSITIVITY (RAMP)

Sets the dynamic threshold. The higher the knob is turned up, the more sensitive Clean becomes. Use lower settings for more subtle results that only affect louder playing, and higher settings for a more responsive spectrum. If ramping is engaged, this knob's function changes to control the ramping speed.

## WET

Controls the loudness of the processed signal. Able to apply a healthy boost.

## DRY

Controls the loudness of the unprocessed signal. Able to apply a healthy boost.

## BYPASS

Tap to turn Clean on/off. Hold to max out Clean's sag effect.

## PRESETS

The left and right positions each store a preset, while the middle position is live (current settings). To save to the right slot, hold the right footswitch for 3 seconds, then add the left footswitch for another 3 seconds. Do the same for the left slot, but start by holding down the left footswitch. The middle LED will blink to indicate success.

## DYNAMICS

Set the amount of compression. You will gradually transition through different zones as the knob is turned up, going from compression, to limiting, to sag.

## ATTACK

Controls how quickly the compression will set in. The minimum position is the fastest with an onset speed of 0.5ms, and at the maximum position the speed is 300ms. The ATTACK knob also controls the speed of the motion-related EQ modes and MOTION mode.

## RELEASE

Controls how quickly the compression and left EQ mode will fade out.
- LEFT position: Fast (50ms)
- MIDDLE position: User adjustable (default 650ms, see Hidden Options)
- RIGHT position: Slow (1.5s)

## PHYSICS

Manipulates the physical behavior of Clean's dynamic response, letting you sabotage it to produce instability and fluctuations.
- LEFT: Subtle, wobbly movement
- MIDDLE: Normal, stable movement
- RIGHT: Twitchy, unstable movement

## EQ

A one-knob EQ with three modes set by the toggle below. The counter-clockwise side of the sweep removes highs, while the clockwise side removes lows. At noon the EQ will have no effect.

## EQ MODE

Selects the EQ's mode:
- LEFT (SHIFTY): Shifts when you play. Whenever your input signal passes the SENSITIVITY threshold the EQ will move away from the EQ knob setting towards full frequency. Speed set by ATTACK and RELEASE controls.
- MIDDLE (MANUAL): Classic, fixed EQ. Rotating clockwise removes lows, rotating counter-clockwise removes highs.
- RIGHT (MODULATED): Modulates when you play. The EQ knob selects the center point that will be modulated around, and the ATTACK knob sets the speed. The motion will smoothly fade away when you stop playing.

## AUX

Engages a swell effect. There are two Swell Modes: Dynamic (default) and Manual. The swell effects are momentary by default, but this can be changed using the LATCH dip switch.

## Visual Assist

The left LED is a helpful guide for dialing in your settings:
- RED LED: Shows how much compression is being applied (brighter = more compression)
- GREEN LED: Indicates you are in a Swell Mode and tracks the rising and falling of swells

# Hidden Options

Hold down both footswitches to access the Hidden Options. Both LEDs will light up green.

## Gate Threshold

Sets the threshold for Clean's noise gate. Any input audio quieter than this threshold will be muted. Controlled by SENSITIVITY knob in Hidden Options mode. The gate is activated by the NOISE GATE dip switch.

## Gate Release

Sets how quickly the Noise Gate will re-engage once you go below the threshold. Controlled by DYNAMICS knob in Hidden Options mode.

## Swell In

Sets how long it takes to reach full volume when Swell is engaged. Controlled by WET knob in Hidden Options mode. Range: 100ms to 4 seconds.

## Swell Out

Sets how long it takes to return to silence when Swell is engaged. Controlled by DRY knob in Hidden Options mode. Range: 100ms to 4 seconds.

## User Release

Allows you to set a custom value for the middle RELEASE position. Controlled by ATTACK knob in Hidden Options mode. Default setting is 650ms. Range: 50ms to 1.5s.

## Envelope Balance

Lets you filter out low frequencies from the audio being used to control Clean's dynamic response. Controlled by EQ knob in Hidden Options mode. You can use this to make Clean more sensitive to the upper register of your instrument, or to go even further and ignore bass sounds (e.g. a kick drum) while still responding to higher frequency sounds (e.g. a snare drum). This affects both the internal envelope follower and the sidechain input. Note: this only filters control signals, you will not hear audible filtering.

## Envelope Type

Selects which type of envelope follower Clean uses. Controlled by RELEASE knob in Hidden Options mode.
- ANALOG: A typical envelope follower that matches the exact settings of the ATTACK and RELEASE controls.
- ADAPTIVE: A reactive envelope follower that dynamically adjusts the attack and release settings depending on the loudness of your playing. The ATTACK and RELEASE controls select the slowest possible speed, and the Adaptive envelope will adjust within that range. Good at staying out of the way and helps faster playing maintain its energy, but not quite as smooth as Analog.
- COMBO: Uses Analog ATTACK and Adaptive RELEASE. A best-of-both approach that gives you the responsiveness of Adaptive with the accuracy of Analog.

## Spread Routing

Lets you independently assign SPREAD to the EQ or volume-based effects (compressor and swell). Options: BOTH, VOLUME, EQ. This is helpful if you want identical compression on both channels but stereo movement from the EQ.

## Reset Hidden Options

To reset all Hidden Options to default: flip the preset toggle to the left position and back to center three times. Once you see the blinking lights, press both footswitches to confirm.

# Compression 101

A compressor's job is simple: If you make a sound that's "too loud" the compressor will turn itself on, catch that sound, and make it quieter before it escapes the pedal.

- SENSITIVITY controls what "too loud" is
- ATTACK controls how quickly the compressor turns on
- RELEASE controls how quickly it turns back off
- DYNAMICS controls how much quieter to make it
- The WET level is important because the more compression you apply, the more you will need to boost to compensate

The result is an overall smoothing effect — instead of deep valleys and peaks you have rolling hills, or flat plains if you want to get really smooshed.

# Compression Ideas



## Transparent Enhancer

Subtle compression (2:1) that thickens, focuses, and extends your sound. The slower ATTACK setting means your pick attack is uncompressed, preserving the energy of your instrument. Suggested usage: Lead lines or a versatile all-around starter.

## Tight & Lively

Medium amount of compression (4:1), with a swift attack and release. Responsive and balanced smoothing that absorbs everything except the first bit of your pick attack, but releases quickly to avoid an overly squished and static feeling. Suggested usage: Chords or fast and dense playing (e.g. funk).

## Parallel Pop

Using the DRY knob to create extreme settings that bring out details and heap on sustain, then blend the uncompressed sound back in to restore character and dynamics. The result is a big, punchy sound that allows for plenty of experimentation. Suggested usage: Bass, drums, or thickening an instrument or mix.

# Dynamics — Two-Stage Compression

Each of Clean's stereo channels is actually two compressors run in series. Stage One is a shape-shifting compressor. Stage Two is a variable limiter. Two-stage compression is a popular studio trick for creating the most musical compression possible. The first stage can be more flexible and relaxed because the second stage will catch anything that slips through.

## Stage One — Primary Compressor

This is Clean's primary compressor. As you move through the DYNAMICS knob sweep it evolves:

COMPRESSION: Throughout the first half of the knob's sweep you will find classic compression that gradually increases in strength (from subtle/transparent to clamped down near noon). Ratio range: 1:1 through 2:1, 4:1, to 10:1. Uses feedback topology.

LIMITING: At noon you enter hard limiting. The SENSITIVITY knob sets the maximum possible loudness — anything louder will be completely squished. You gradually blend in feedforward limiting as you push further.
- Feedback: Natural, smooth, relaxed
- Feedforward: Precise, snappy, aggressive
The limiting gets progressively tighter until feedforward takes over completely.

SAG: The last portion of the DYNAMICS range goes beyond compression into a state that simulates an overloaded tube. Your signal falls out, falters, and sputters as you play harder.

## Stage Two — Automatic Limiter

Clean's second stage is a straightforward hard limiter. It reads the settings of the primary compressor and follows behind, catching any transients or peaks that get by the first stage.

## Physical Modeling

The PHYSICS toggle models the physical response of a spring to interfere with the envelope's ability to follow you accurately, creating organic wobbles and bursts of motion.

# EQ — Dynamic EQ

Clean includes a dynamic EQ that reacts to your playing. It has three modes that each offer their own way of sculpting sound, introducing motion, and generating stereo nuance. The EQ knob is a two-way control that adjusts either lows or highs.

MANUAL: A classic, fixed EQ. Clockwise removes lows, counter-clockwise removes highs.

MODULATED: An EQ that modulates when you play. The EQ knob selects the center point, and the ATTACK knob sets the speed. Motion smoothly fades away when you stop playing.

SHIFTY: An EQ that shifts when you play. Whenever your input passes the SENSITIVITY threshold, the EQ moves away from the knob setting towards full frequency. Speed set by ATTACK and RELEASE controls.

Stereo movement with SPREAD: Manual mode creates bursts of flickering panning, Modulated mode auto-pans side to side, and Shifty introduces smooth dynamic panning.

In all modes, setting the knob to noon deactivates the EQ.

# Swell

Clean gives you different ways to access a softer world. Swells are engaged by the AUX footswitch. The footswitch is momentary by default, but can be changed with the LATCH dip switch. Left LED turns green when Swell is engaged.

Dynamic Swell (default): Signal swells in whenever you play something louder than the SENSITIVITY threshold and swells back out once playing goes under the threshold.

Manual Swell: You only experience a swell when you press the AUX footswitch. Holding the footswitch mutes your signal; releasing it swells back to full volume. Tap once to trigger an instant swell.

Swell speeds are set by hidden options: WET adjusts swell in speed, DRY adjusts swell out speed.

BLIPS: Set both SWELL IN and SWELL OUT to fastest speed to turn playing into short muted blips with a soft but percussive character.

# Dusty

Dusty mode is a hidden bonus activated by the DUSTY dip switch. Clean's limiter typically hangs in the background as a final stage to smooth everything out. Dusty mode sets the limiter loose, turning it into a tactile overdrive with soft edges and a crumbly decay.

Clean's limiter is actually a clipping circuit with a wide range, variable threshold. Dusty mode lets you set the threshold so low that it rips into your sound and distorts it. SENSITIVITY sets that threshold and works loosely as an "amount" control.

Signal flow: COMPRESSOR → EQ → MIXER → LIMITER. Everything is before the limiter, so anything you change on the pedal will influence the distortion. Turning up DYNAMICS reduces distortion because it compresses audio before it hits the limiter.

Tip: Crank DYNAMICS, boost WET to compensate, then roll back SENSITIVITY for blooming overdrive that sputters and breaks when you dig in. Your dry signal will also be affected — turn up DRY and the dust will emerge.

# Customize — Dip Switches

The blue-labeled dip switches on top of Clean allow you to engage alternate behaviors. Dip switch settings are saved with your presets.

## MISO

Mono In, Stereo Out. Splits a mono input signal into a stereo output signal.

## SPREAD

Engages stereo processing. The EQ, compressor, and swells each have their own distinct type of stereo motion. SPREAD also makes the dynamic response of the left and right channels independent, so that each side experiences its own unique compression and envelope response.

## LATCH

Changes the hold function for each footswitch from momentary to latching, so it will remain engaged until the footswitch is held again.

## SIDECHAIN

Allows you to use an external audio signal to trigger the compression, useful for syncing to drums and other instruments. When engaged, the compressor will follow the signal plugged into the 1/8" SIDECHAIN input on the side of the pedal instead of the input audio.

## NOISE GATE

Activates a noise gate that mutes the input when you're not playing. Filters out hum and other noise amplified through compression. Hidden options under SENSITIVITY set the gate threshold, and DYNAMICS sets its release time.

## MOTION

Engages a special "motion mode" that modulates the amount of compression being applied. DYNAMICS sets the depth of modulation, ATTACK sets the rate. Motion only occurs while playing and gently fades out when input goes below the sensitivity threshold.

## SWELL AUX

Changes the function of the AUX footswitch to allow for manual swells. Clean triggers a single swell whenever AUX is pressed. Holding AUX mutes your signal, swelling back in upon release.

## DUSTY

Transforms the limiter inside Clean into a distinct form of crumbly overdrive. The limiter is at the very end of Clean's signal chain and affects both wet and dry signals.

# Ramping

Ramping gives you the ability to automate Clean's knobs, either as a one-time movement (Ramp) or continuous motion (Bounce).

Bounce: Modulates a knob continuously like a tremolo. The position of the knob sets the maximum or minimum point of the range (depending on SWEEP setting).

Ramp: Movement only happens once when you turn the pedal on. Your chosen knob(s) either rise or fall to the position set by the knob, then stay there. Useful for creating a wave of motion when you first turn Clean on.

Setup:
1. Engage Bounce or Ramp via dip switches
2. Choose which knob(s) to control: DYNAMICS, ATTACK, EQ, DRY, WET
3. Choose the sweep: (B)ottom or (T)op
4. Set the speed via SENSITIVITY (RAMP) knob

Ramping is engaged as soon as the dip switch for a corresponding knob is set to ON. The SENSITIVITY knob automatically changes to control ramp speed. You can still adjust SENSITIVITY while ramping by holding down the left footswitch as you move the knob.

# External Control

Clean is compatible with MIDI, CV, expression, and external footswitch.

CV and expression are set up the same way as ramping using the dip switches. The pedal detects a CV or expression signal when you plug it in and hands over control. If you plug in a CV or expression signal but engage none of the knobs, you will have control over SENSITIVITY.

MIDI lets you control everything, including Hidden Options and dip switches. Requires a Chase Bliss MIDIbox to convert the signal to a 1/4" TRS jack.

The MIDI jack can also be used to control the AUX footswitch with an external normally-open momentary footswitch using a TS cable.

Setup:
1. Choose which knob(s) to control
2. Choose the sweep
3. Choose the polarity
4. Set the range

Connections:
- CV: Floating ring TRS to TS cable (0-5V range; higher or negative voltage could damage the pedal)
- EXP: TRS cable
- SIDECHAIN: TS cable
- MIDI: TRS cable
- AUX: TS cable and normally open switch

# Signal Flow

Signal path per channel:
LEFT/RIGHT IN → PRE-GAIN → PRE-EMPHASIS → COMPRESSOR → EQ → MIXER (wet/dry) → DE-EMPHASIS → LIMITER → LEFT/RIGHT OUT

Additional routing:
- ENVELOPE FOLLOWER fed from input to CONTROL CENTER
- MISO splits mono input to stereo
- SIDECHAIN IN feeds the envelope follower
- DRY path bypasses compressor and EQ, goes through its own PRE-GAIN, PRE-EMPHASIS, DE-EMPHASIS, and level control
- WET LEVEL and DRY LEVEL controls feed into MIXER
- Both wet and dry paths pass through the final LIMITER stage
