# Introduction

TX-6 is an ultra-portable 6 channel stereo recording mixer, as well as a 12 channel USB audio interface, and the first release in the teenage engineering field system. With more features than first meets the eye, it's a most diverse audio device, in and outside the studio. Updates are constantly made for this product and an up-to-date manual can be found online at teenage.engineering/guides/tx-6.

# Field System

TX-6 is a highly technical and delicate product. Register your unit at teenage.engineering/register. Field system began as an idea to create a series of products that function as part of a whole, with aluminium casings and nylon bag accessories. Every item is designed with portability, compatibility and durability in mind.

# Hardware Overview

Key features:
- Ultra-portable pro mixer
- 6 stereo inputs (3.5 mm mini jack)
- 3 stereo outputs (main 6.35 mm / cue 3.5 mm / aux 3.5 mm)
- USB audio / MIDI via USB-C
- 3-band equalizer (±18 dB per band)
- Low-pass & high-pass filter
- 2 stereo multi-effect units (FX I send, FX II insert)
- Compressor
- Limiter
- Crossfader (DJ mode)
- MIDI over USB-C and Bluetooth LE
- Instrument tuner
- Built-in synthesizer with sequencer
- 8-hour rechargeable battery
- Made in durable aluminium with PU synthetic leather backing and custom knobs and faders
- 48x64 pixel monochrome OLED display

# Audio Inputs

Six 3.5 mm stereo mini jack input sockets located on the top side of the unit. Each input can be individually configured as mono, stereo, or split L+R in any combination, supporting up to 6 stereo sources or 12 mono sources across the six physical inputs. The USB Type-C port can also be used as a multi-channel audio interface, as well as for MIDI control, firmware updates, and charging.

When a plug is connected, TX-6 detects it and shows a dialog to select configuration:
- Mono: left input signal mixed to both channels. Turn one extra step to enable balanced mono input mode.
- Stereo: default, left and right stereo mix.
- Split: left and right signals combined for use with a splitter cable; the pan parameter controls the balance of the two signals.

# Audio Outputs

Three output sockets located on the bottom side of the unit:
- Aux out: 3.5 mm stereo mini jack
- Cue out: 3.5 mm headset and mic mini jack
- Main out: 6.35 mm stereo jack (includes removable 6.35 mm to 3.5 mm adapter)

Outputs can be connected to headphones, studio monitors, PA speakers, or other audio equipment. Main out is used for the main audio output signal. Aux and cue out can be used for monitoring, recording, and creating effect loops. USB can be used for multitrack recording to a DAW.

Since the aux jack delivers a stronger output signal than the cue jack, their functions can be switched (e.g., to drive high-impedance headphones).

## Audio Connection Examples

Inputs: Pocket operators, OP-1, OP-Z, or any line-level sources. Pocket Operator Modular should be connected via its speaker output. Guitars and microphones may need a preamp. Cue out directly accepts a headset microphone such as the M-1. USB can also be used for audio input.

Outputs: Connect to M-1 headset, OB-4 speaker, DAW for multitrack recording, or other audio equipment.

# Power and Charging

Turn on by flipping the power switch to the up position. The LED next to the switch lights up to indicate the unit is on. The display shows the TX-6 title screen before arriving at the home screen showing main level, VU meters, battery level, and USB charge indicator.

The unit is charged through the USB port. Battery level is indicated on the home screen and from the system menu. When charging with the unit turned off, press any button to show battery level. The side LED turns green when fully charged. To keep the battery healthy, charge at least every 6 months.

# Firmware Update

To update firmware:
1. Connect TX-6 via USB to a computer.
2. Hold the cue button while switching power on to enter firmware update mode.
3. The device will show up as a mass storage disk.
4. Put the new firmware file on the disk and eject. Wait for the update to finish.

Latest firmware: teenage.engineering/downloads

# Controls and Navigation

The main control centers around the select knob and button:
- Rotate select knob: adjust main volume, parameter values, navigate menu
- Press select button: select and confirm choices
- Shift button: exit any menu and return to home screen; used in combination with other buttons for additional functions

Control elements:
- FX I and FX II buttons
- 6 track buttons
- Aux button
- Cue button
- 3 parameter knobs per track (blue, orange, white)
- 6 faders with LED VU meters

The faders control the volume of each track. Input level is shown on the LED VU meters. If clipping occurs, all five LEDs turn red. If a channel is configured as joined mono input, the fader controls both L and R signals.

# Screen and Display

Home screen displays main VU meters, battery charge status, and main volume setting. If any functionality is activated or a knob is turned, that is reflected on the display before returning to the previous screen. After a minute of inactivity the display dims automatically. If left untouched longer, the display turns off. Press any button to reactivate.

Display brightness can be adjusted from the system menu. Theme can be toggled between light and dark mode.

# General Operations

- Solo/mute: press track buttons (when not controlling another feature)
- Cycle through track menu: hold shift + press track button repeatedly
- Show aux/cue page: press aux or cue button
- Show mic page: hold shift + press cue button
- Show system menu: hold shift + press select
- Activate effect: press FX I or FX II button
- Show FX menu: hold shift + press FX button
- Main volume: turn select from home screen
- Tempo: press select from home screen
- Exit any menu: press shift
- Reset parameter to default: press and hold select button for one second

# Track Menu

Hold shift and press a track button to show the track menu. Press several times while holding shift to cycle between settings. Unless a setting is assigned to a parameter knob, it can be adjusted by turning select. A symbol indicates which knob a parameter is assigned to. If there are multiple settings on a page, press select to move to the next. Press shift to exit.

Track menu pages:
- 3-band equalizer (high/mid/low)
- Low-pass / high-pass filter
- Compressor
- Input gain
- Panning (left and right)

## Equalizer

The parameter knobs control a 3-band equalizer by default with ±18 dB gain per band:
- Blue knob: high frequencies
- Orange knob: mid frequencies
- White knob: low frequencies

Use the EQ to adjust individual track frequencies for a well-balanced mix.

# Solo and Mute Modes

When track buttons are not controlling a specific feature, they are used for solo. Four solo modes (configurable from system menu):
- Solo: mutes other tracks
- Mute: mutes active track
- Solo momentary: momentarily solos selected channel and mutes others
- Mute momentary: momentarily mutes selected channel

# Parameter Knob Settings

A custom knob scheme can be configured from the 'pots' setting in the system menu. Individual configurations can be set per track. When a parameter is assigned to a knob, it cannot be controlled from the track menu.

Available assignable parameters:
- flt: high-pass / low-pass filter
- comp: compressor
- pan: panning
- eq h: equalizer high
- eq m: equalizer mid
- eq l: equalizer low
- aux: aux send
- FX I: FX I send
- freq: oscillator frequency
- len: sequencer note length

Modes:
- Custom: a configurable scheme for all potentiometers
- Track: individual potentiometer settings per track
- Synth: dedicated synth controls for the built-in synthesizer and drum machine

# Effects - FX I (Send Effects)

FX I is a send effect where some of the signal from a track is sent to the effect before being returned. When the FX I page is active:
- Hold a track button and turn select to adjust send level per track
- Hold shift and turn select to adjust FX I return level

FX I effects:
- Chorus: subtle, medium, strong
- Reverb: drone, light, small, medium, large
- Delay: 1/8, 1/4, 2/3, 3/4, 1, 3/2
- Delay: warp
- Delay: mash

Tip: When on the FX screen, hold the cue button to preview the effect without activating it on the main output.

# Effects - FX II (Insert Effects)

FX II is an insert effect where the entire signal passes through when activated. FX II can operate on a single track or on the main mix. Click any track button to select a track; click the selected track again to select main mode.

FX II effects:
- Tremolo
- Freeze (tap select to reset parameter)
- Tape: stop 1, stop 2, pong
- Filter
- Distortion: blow, drive, push, dstroy
- Crush: chip, bend, shape, bit

# Tempo and Clock

TX-6 has an internal tempo settable locally or controlled by external MIDI clock. Tempo range: 40–400 BPM.

Access: press select from home screen, or via system menu, or quick access.
- Turn select to adjust tempo
- FX I button for tap tempo
- FX II button to set a new start point
- Press select to toggle play/stop (starts internal sequencer if active in synth mode)

Tempo is used for beat-synced effects (tremolo, delay) and the built-in sequencer.

Clock sources: internal, USB, BLE. MIDI clock output can be toggled on/off. If both input and output are enabled, MIDI clock messages are routed between USB and BLE.

# Quick Access

Some functions can be reached through quick access. Press select from the home screen to reach the currently assigned function. Hold shift and turn select to switch between quick access functions (tempo and recorder). Tap shift to exit back to home screen.

# Disk Recorder

TX-6 can record and play back 48 kHz 24-bit PCM stereo WAV files using a USB mass storage device (USB flash drive or powered SSD). The device must be formatted with the FAT file system using Master Boot Record (not GUID).

Access: select 'disk' from main menu or use quick access.

Controls:
- FX I: play
- FX II: record
- Shift + record: auto mode (recording starts when audio is detected, stops after 5 seconds of silence)
- Select: show file browser; scroll through files
- FX I in browser: start playback
- Hold select in file browser: delete file (confirm by turning and clicking select)
- Turn select during playback: scrub (stops playback)

Do not disconnect the USB drive when the disk is busy (indicated by a flashing symbol on home screen). Free space is shown in GB/MB and HH:MM from the USB screen in the system menu.

# Aux Out

Aux out can be used as a second output. Press the aux button to show the aux page with a graph of aux send levels per track.

Controls:
- Select: control aux output volume
- Hold track button + turn select: adjust aux send level per track
- Press select: access 'mode' setting (pre-fader or post-fader)

Aux out can create external effects loops routed back into an input, or connect to a recording device for live performance recording.

# Cue Out

Cue out can be used as a headset output. Press the cue button to show the cue page.

Controls:
- Track buttons: assign which tracks to send to cue out
- Select: adjust cue volume
- Press select: access 'mode' setting (cue or main)
  - Cue: only selected tracks
  - Main: hear exactly what goes through main out

# Headset Microphone

When a headset with microphone is connected to cue out:
- Hold shift + cue button to access mic controls
- Press cue button and turn select to access options

Mic settings:
- On/Off: click to activate, turn to set volume
- Gain: 0 to +31 dB
- Gate: mic gate on/off
- Duck: ducking on/off
- FX: mic effects (none, reverb with wetness 0–100, delay with time 0–100)

# Instrument Tuner

TX-6 features a tuner for checking the pitch of any incoming audio signal. Choose the input track by turning the select knob or using the track buttons. The pitch is automatically detected and displayed.

# Sync Output

TX-6 can output sync signals over both aux and cue outputs for syncing pocket operators, modular systems, or other compatible devices. The left channel carries the sync signal; the right channel carries the audio signal. The aux output has the strongest signal.

To enable: press aux or cue to access the page, then hold shift and turn select to enable the desired sync signal.

Sync modes:
- 8th note sync (great for pocket operators)
- 16th note sync (great for modular synths)

# USB Audio Settings

The USB setting in the system menu selects USB audio routing options:
- 2 ch: 2+2 channel interface, main mix send and receive
- 12 in: 12-channel USB input mixed with analog inputs, main mix stereo output
- 12 out: 12-channel USB output post-faders, stereo input to main (great for multitracking to a DAW)

Additional settings (press select):
- Main out source: ext (USB input overrides main output) or mix (signals mixed together)
- Output volume: adjusts volume of main mix USB output to prevent clipping

Sample rate options:
- Default: 48 kHz only (highest quality)
- Multi: 48 kHz / 44.1 kHz (lets audio host choose, for extended compatibility)

TX-6 supports USB audio in 24-bit format. TX-6 is MFi certified and can connect directly to iPhone using a standard USB-C to Lightning cable (no camera connection kit required).

# System Menu

Access: hold shift + press select. Turn knob to scroll, click select to choose a setting, turn select to adjust values, press select to advance to next setting on the page, press shift to exit.

System menu items include:
- VU meter: pre-fader or post-fader metering
- Limiter: on/off (prevents clipping on master mix)
- Memory presets: when activated, FX buttons store/recall track settings (EQ and filter). Hold shift + hold FX I or II to save; press FX button to recall (active only while held)
- USB audio routing
- LED brightness: three intensity levels
- Display brightness
- Theme: light or dark mode
- BLE (Bluetooth): enable/disable, device or host mode
- Name: rename your TX-6 for BLE identification
- Scene: save and recall all settings in three scenes (A, B, C); can also be loaded via MIDI program change
- OS version information
- Battery level/charging display
- GND: eliminate USB ground loop noise
- Factory reset: turn clockwise to confirm
- Pots: parameter knob configuration
- Solo mode selection
- MIDI settings
- Sample rate
- Disk recorder
- Tempo/clock settings

# Synth Mode

In synth mode, each channel becomes a configurable sound generator mixed with any input signal.

Modes:
- Off: synth mode is off
- Play: trigger sounds using the track buttons
- Seq: playing a sequenced pattern selectable from track settings
- Tone: oscillators always on

Per-track synth settings (hold shift + press track button repeatedly):
- Sound: select waveform or synthesis sound
- Frequency: adjust synthesis frequency/tone
- Length: adjust synthesis length/decay
- Seq: select sequencer pattern

Available waveforms: sine, triangle, square, saw
Available synthesis sounds: kick drum, snare drum, hand clap, hi-hat

# DJ Mode

DJ mode turns TX-6 on its side for use as a DJ mixer. When activated, channel 1 is disabled and its fader crossfades between channel 5 and channel 6 inputs.

Crossfade modes:
- Off: crossfade disabled
- Slow fade: gradual DJ-style crossfade between channels 5 and 6
- Fast cut: quick DJ-style crossfade between channels 5 and 6

Connect headphones to cue output for monitoring. Rotate the mixer 90 degrees for the DJ feel.

# MIDI

TX-6 can send and receive MIDI control change (CC) messages over USB and BLE interfaces, as well as MIDI clock and transport control.

MIDI settings:
- In: enables MIDI CC and program change input to TX-6
- Out: sends MIDI CC out of TX-6 whenever a knob, fader, or button is moved or pressed
- Note On: enables incoming MIDI notes for use with the built-in synthesizer
- Note Off / MIDI Off: disables all incoming and outgoing MIDI

When using external MIDI control, internal knobs and faders can be disabled with the MIDI command 'local control off'. Scenes can be loaded via MIDI program change messages when MIDI input is enabled. See the online guide for a detailed description of the latest MIDI implementation.

# Signal Flow

Overview of audio signal paths from input to output:

Input channels: ADC → gain → compressor → filter → EQ → fader/solo → pre-VU-meter → FX1 bus / FX2 bus / master bus / aux pre-send / aux post-send / cue track enable → post-VU-meter → pan/balance → stereo/mono

Master: FX1 bus → FX1 level → master mix; FX2 bus → FX2 level → master mix; master bus → master mix → limiter → master volume → VU meter → main out; aux pre-bus / aux post-bus → aux mode → aux volume → aux out

Headphones: HP cue bus / HP master bus → HP mode → HP volume → cue out; VU meter

Microphone: ADC → mic gain → mic enable → mic FX → volume → master bus; VU meter

# Bluetooth LE

TX-6 can accept connections from Bluetooth LE MIDI devices. Bluetooth is disabled by default; enable BLE in the system menu.

Modes:
- Device: BLE is on and accepting connection requests from BLE MIDI hosts
- Host: scanning for BLE MIDI devices, connects to the one with the strongest signal

If multiple TX-6 units are nearby, rename your device from the 'name' system menu to avoid confusion. The BLE device name is the advertised name over the BLE protocol.

Bluetooth frequency band: 2400–2483.5 MHz, maximum output power: 10 dBm EIRP.

# Care and Maintenance

Clean the shell with a microfibre cloth. Use pressurized air to remove dust from the faders. Charge the battery at least every 6 months to keep it healthy; if not used for a long time, it may not charge again.

# Warnings and Safety

Risk of explosion or fire if the battery is replaced with an incorrect type. Only a battery supplied by teenage engineering and installed by qualified personnel should be used. To prevent possible hearing damage, do not listen at high sound levels for long periods. Store small parts out of reach of children and infants. Do not try to charge or use a unit with a seemingly damaged battery. Do not dispose of battery into fire, hot oven, or mechanically crush or cut. Do not leave battery in extremely high temperature environments.
