# Introduction

Ableton Move is a compact and portable standalone instrument that can be used to capture and perform spontaneous compositions on the go, making it the ideal tool for quick and intuitive music creation. Move is designed to easily integrate with other Ableton products, which means that you can start a Set on Move and continue working on it whether you are using Note, Live, or Push. You can also use Move as a control surface for Live or connect it to class-compliant MIDI devices in order to send or receive MIDI data.

## What's in the Box?

Move comes with a USB-C power supply, USB-C cable, getting started guide, safety and warranty information booklet, and controls overview.

## Hardware Overview

32 velocity-sensitive, backlit soft silicone pads. Touch-sensitive, clickable wheel. 9 high-resolution, touch-sensitive encoders. 16 backlit multifunctional buttons for sequencing and accessing secondary features. 20 backlit buttons for accessing primary features such as navigation, recording or looping. 1.3" white OLED display (128×64). Stereo line out (3.5 mm jack). Stereo line in (3.5 mm jack). Built-in microphone. Built-in speaker. Wi-Fi module. Quad-core ARM Cortex-A72 processor with 2 GB RAM. 64 GB built-in SD Card. USB-A port. USB-C port. Rechargeable battery.

### Controls

Power Button: Press to turn Move on and off. Audio Output: Connect Move to active speakers/monitors or headphones with a 3.5 mm stereo jack connector. Audio Input: Connect an external sound source to Move for sampling using a 3.5 mm stereo jack connector. USB-A: Connect an external MIDI device using a USB-A cable. USB-C: Connect Move to the power supply or your computer using the USB-C cable.

### Connections

1. Power Button: Press to turn Move on and off. 2. Audio Output: 3.5 mm stereo jack connector for speakers/monitors or headphones. 3. Audio Input: 3.5 mm stereo jack connector for external sound sources. 4. USB-A: For external MIDI devices. 5. USB-C: For power supply or computer connection.

# Setup

Move is designed primarily for use as a standalone instrument, but can also be connected to a computer for use as a control surface for Live or as an audio interface. Press the yellow power button on the back of the device to turn Move on. You can use the included power supply or connect Move to a computer via the included USB-C cable to charge the unit. The first time you turn it on, Move will boot up in Standalone Mode with a short onboarding tutorial. To turn Move off, first press the power button, then press the wheel. Press and hold the power button for ten seconds to force Move to turn off.

## Setup Menu

The Setup menu allows you to manage Move's connectivity, update its software, switch to Control Live Mode, reset Move, and more. Open the Setup menu by pressing Shift and then the Step 2 button. Turn the wheel to scroll through the available entries, then press the wheel to open the currently highlighted entry. To exit the Setup menu, press the Back button from an entry or press it twice from a submenu. Alternatively, hold the Back button to exit.

### Battery Status

You can check Move's battery level and status through the Battery submenu entry. Generally, the battery will last for up to four hours of music making. When the battery is running low, a warning message will be shown on Move's display. If the battery runs out, the current Set will be automatically saved and the hardware will shut down safely.

### Connect to Wi-Fi

When Move is connected to Wi-Fi, you can update Move's software directly from the hardware, access Move Manager without plugging Move into a computer, and use Move with Ableton Cloud, as well as enable Link for wireless use. To connect Move to Wi-Fi, select the Wi-Fi submenu entry and open it by pressing the wheel. Select the Wi-Fi Off entry with the wheel and then press the wheel to switch to Wi-Fi On. Turn the wheel to scroll through available networks, then press the wheel to select a network. For password-protected networks, turn the wheel to navigate between characters and press it to enter a character. Holding the Shift button temporarily switches to uppercase letters. It is not possible to access the Wi-Fi submenu when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### Update Move's Software

You can update Move's software to the latest version directly from the hardware. An asterisk is displayed next to the Update entry name whenever a new software update is available. Connect Move to Wi-Fi and open the Update submenu entry. Select Install, press the wheel to confirm, then follow the instructions. By default, you will be notified when a new software update is available. You can toggle notifications off via the Notify On entry and manually check using Check Update. You can check the current version through the Current Version entry.

### Ableton Cloud Status

Enabling Ableton Cloud on Move lets you sync Sets to Note, Live, or Push. The Cloud entry indicates whether Ableton Cloud is currently active. Open the entry to view the hostname for accessing Move Manager, where you can activate or deactivate Cloud. The Cloud entry is not available when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### Switch to Control Live Mode

To use Move as a control surface for Live, select and open the Control Live entry using the wheel, connect Move to your computer via the USB-C cable, and then open Live. Control Live Mode is supported for use with Live 12, version 12.1 or later, as well as Live 11, version 11.3.35 or later. Once in Control Live Mode, the Control Live entry is replaced by the Standalone entry.

### Link

You can enable Link on Move to stay in sync with other Link-connected devices on the same network. Select and open the Link entry to choose between three options: Off, Tempo and Start/Stop. This entry is not available when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### MIDI

Use the MIDI entry to configure how Move sends or receives MIDI data to and from class-compliant MIDI devices. This entry is not available when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### USB-C Audio

You can use Move's USB-C connection to output audio to a computer, smartphone, or tablet. Select and open the USB-C Audio entry to choose which of Move's audio channels to send out. You can choose to send the signal from Move's built-in microphone or audio input (Mic), or the signal from Move's main output (Main Out). By default, Move's audio output is set to Mic. When Main Out is selected, Move's speakers will be disabled. This entry is not available when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### Accessing Move Manager

In the Move Manager submenu, you can access and configure the hostname used for establishing a connection between Move and Move Manager. Each Move unit requires a unique name to distinguish it from other units connected to the same Wi-Fi network. You can use the move.local entry to manually change the name. An asterisk next to the Move Manager submenu entry indicates that the name has changed. The Move Manager submenu is not available when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### Advanced Settings

In the Advanced submenu you can enable anonymous error reporting, as well as reset Move to its factory settings. By default, Move sends crash and error reports to Ableton. This data is anonymous. Resetting Move will delete all Sets, samples, recordings, and settings stored on the unit. This action cannot be undone. The software version installed before the reset will not be reverted, and any Move Sets stored in Ableton Cloud will not be affected. The Advanced Settings submenu is not available when Move is in Control Live Mode.

### Adjust Brightness

You can change the brightness of Move's button and pad LEDs through the Setup menu's Brightness entry. Turn the wheel to switch between four levels of brightness. Setting the brightness to Max only boosts the brightness of the pad LEDs when compared to High, while all other settings affect both pad and button LEDs. This setting does not affect the brightness of the display.

# Move Manager

Move Manager is a web-based file management tool for Move. It allows you to manage the content stored on the hardware: Sets, samples, recordings, and presets, as well as Sets stored in Ableton Cloud. You can also use Move Manager to update Move's software.

## Connecting to Move Manager

To access Move Manager, first turn Move on and either connect it to the same Wi-Fi network as your computer or mobile device, or connect it to your computer via the USB-C port. On Windows 10, if you want to use USB-C to connect to Move Manager, you will first need to update Move to version 1.1 or higher via Wi-Fi. Supported web browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. You can find the hostname in the Setup's Move Manager submenu by opening the How to access? entry.

## Authenticating Move Manager

Enter the hostname in a web browser to connect to Move Manager. When you first open Move Manager, a confirmation code will appear on Move's display, which must be entered in the browser to authenticate the connection. Move Manager can only be authenticated in Standalone Mode. Once authenticated, you will be able to use it even when Move is in Control Live Mode.

## Using Move Manager

After authentication, the home page displays entries for accessing the Sets, Recordings, Samples, Presets, and Settings pages, and a language chooser. The Move icon and menu are shared between pages. On mobile, the menu entries are contained within a collapsible menu.

## Sets Page

The Sets page contains all the Sets available on Move, listed with the Set name, last modified date, and size. Options include download, rename, delete, or export a Set as an audio file. You can sort Sets in ascending or descending order by name, date, or size.

### Downloading a Set

Sets are downloaded as .ablbundle files. On Chrome, an 'Insecure download blocked' message may appear because Move Manager uses a .local domain; select 'Keep' to bypass. Move Sets can be opened in Live 12 or Live 11 (version 11.2 or later) by double-clicking the .ablbundle file. Drum Racks in Move contain Drum Sampler (introduced in Live 12.1); opening in earlier versions converts Drum Sampler to Simpler. Sets can also be opened in Note on iOS/iPadOS. AirDrop can be used to share Sets between Apple devices.

### Renaming a Set

Click on the rename icon. A dialog will open where you can enter the new name. Click Rename to confirm or Cancel to exit.

### Additional Set Options in Move Manager

Click on the ellipsis icon to access additional options: deleting Sets or exporting them as audio files. Export supports WAV or MP3 format. All clips in the Set will be exported, ordered from left to right. Deleting a Set from Move Manager cannot be undone.

## Recordings Page

The Recordings page gives an overview of all audio files recorded on Move, listed by name, recording date, and size. You can sort, play back, download, rename, or delete recordings. Deleting a recording cannot be undone.

## Samples Page

The Samples page allows you to upload samples to Move, view uploaded samples, and organize them into folders. Samples are listed by name, upload date, size, and file type. Each Move Set can use up to 400 MB of samples. Move stores samples in memory as 32-bit, so the 400 MB limit corresponds to approximately 20 minutes of stereo or 40 minutes of mono sample time. Supported upload formats: WAV, AIF, or AIFF files that are 100 MB or less.

### Uploading Samples and Sample Folders

Upload methods: Click the Upload button and select files; use the Upload button's drop-down menu; or drag and drop files onto the Samples page. You can upload entire sample folders through the 'Upload folder' option or by dragging and dropping. Uploading folders with the Upload button only works in Chrome, Firefox, or Edge (not Safari). If a folder contains a mix of supported and unsupported files, only supported files will be included. When uploading with the same name as an existing item, a warning message prevents accidental overwriting.

### Organizing Samples

You can create folders in the Samples page and upload samples into them. It is not possible to reorganize existing content by dragging and dropping within the Samples page. To add content to an existing folder, open the folder first and then upload directly. Nested folders are supported.

## Presets Page

The Presets page allows you to upload Track Presets (.ablpresetbundle files) to Move, view presets saved on Move or uploaded via Move Manager, and create folders. When you upload a Drum Rack preset, all included samples are automatically added to the Samples page in a dedicated Preset Samples folder. Organization works the same as for samples.

## Settings Page

The Settings page includes information about storage space available on Move and the software version installed, as well as options to activate Ableton Cloud or update Move's software. An asterisk appears next to Settings when a new software version is available. Updating from Move Manager via USB-C does not require Wi-Fi.

### Activating Ableton Cloud on Move

Click the Activate Cloud button in the Ableton Cloud section of the Settings page. You will be redirected to ableton.com to log in and accept the Ableton Cloud Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Once activated, any Sets previously synced with Cloud will automatically appear on Move. To disable Cloud, click Deactivate Cloud; previously synced Sets will not be removed from the Set Overview.

## Advanced Page

The Advanced page contains device logs for troubleshooting. Access it via move.local/advanced. Download device log files from the Logs tab.

# Connectivity

Move is designed as a portable instrument with connectivity features that expand beyond standalone use. You can sample your environment, connect MIDI devices, jam with other musicians via Link-enabled apps and devices, or upload Sets to Cloud.

## Hardware Connectivity

Move includes a built-in speaker, audio output (3.5 mm, line out and headphone out), built-in microphone and audio input (3.5 mm, line-level signals). Supports sampling via USB-C and can be used as an audio interface with Live. Can connect to MIDI devices through USB-A port. The input works with line-level signals; for microphones or guitar pickups, use an external preamp with line-level output.

### Using Move as an Audio Interface

When connected to a computer via USB-C, Move can function as an audio interface by routing audio over the USB connection. In Standalone Mode, Move uses a 44,100 Hz sample rate. You can send and receive audio between Move and a connected computer. In Control Live Mode, only Move's audio input can be sent to a computer. To use Move as an audio interface on Windows, install Live 12.1 or download the driver from the Download Archive.

### Connecting MIDI Devices

Move can send and receive MIDI notes to and from class-compliant USB-powered MIDI devices via the USB-A port. Low-speed and full-speed USB devices are supported, but not hi-speed USB devices such as Push. Move can supply up to 500 mA of power. Use the MIDI entry in the Setup menu to toggle between MIDI In Only and MIDI Out Only. Move can receive polyphonic aftertouch messages; monophonic aftertouch, MIDI CC, and MIDI mapping are not supported. Sending and receiving MIDI data does not work in Control Live Mode.

## Using Ableton Link on Move

Ableton Link keeps devices in time over a wired or wireless network. Link is built into Move, Note, Live and Push, as well as iOS applications. To activate Link, connect Move to the same network as other devices. Open the Link entry in the Setup menu and choose: Off, Tempo (syncs tempo only), or Start/Stop (syncs playback position and tempo). The first device to join sets the initial tempo. Any Link-enabled device can change tempo and all others will follow. A count-in cannot be used for recording when Link is enabled.

## Using Ableton Cloud on Move

Ableton Cloud lets you store up to eight Sets from Move and Note, accessible across all Ableton products. Enable Cloud via Move Manager. Synced Sets feature a small cloud icon. To upload a Set: hold Shift, press the Set's pad, select Upload to Cloud. Move must be connected to Wi-Fi to upload. To remove a Set from Cloud: hold Shift, press the pad, select Make Local. This removes from other Cloud-enabled devices but retains a local copy. Synced Sets stay up to date between Note and Move. Move supports a maximum of four tracks, so only the first four tracks from synced Note Sets will be available.

# Navigating Between Set Modes

Move has three main modes: Set Overview, Note Mode, and Session Mode. Set Overview contains all Sets; access from other modes by holding Shift and pressing Step 1. Note Mode is for building Sets (record, capture, edit clips, play and sequence notes). Session Mode is for arranging, copying, and launching clips. Switch between Note and Session Mode using the Note/Session toggle. Hold the toggle to briefly preview the other mode without switching.

# Set Overview

The Set Overview is shown when Move is first powered on and contains all Sets including four Demo Sets and Cloud-synced Sets. You can save up to 32 Sets. Each pad functions as a slot. Colored pads represent existing Sets. A pulsing pad specifies the currently selected Set. Unlit pads indicate empty slots. A white pad indicates a selected empty slot. Sets are automatically saved as you make music, when you enter the Set Overview, and when you shut down.

## Set Options in the Overview

Press a pad to select a Set. Press Play to preview. To adjust Set volume, hold the Set's pad and turn the Volume encoder. Alternatively, turn the Volume encoder without pressing a pad to adjust overall output level.

### Creating New Sets

Press an empty pad, then press any track button to enter Note Mode. Newly created Sets are populated with four random Track Presets: Drums (top track), Bass (second), Pad/Synth Keys/Plucked/Rhythmic (third), Synth Lead/Winds (bottom). These presets can be customized and swapped.

### Changing a Pad's Color

Hold Shift and press the Set's pad. Select the Color entry, then turn the wheel to choose a color. The pad LED updates instantly. Press Back to return.

### Copying Sets

Hold the Copy button and press the pad you want to duplicate. Then press the destination pad. If the destination already contains a Set, a confirmation message appears. Overwriting cannot be undone. Press Copy again to cancel before pasting.

### Deleting Sets

Hold the Delete button and press the pad containing the Set. A confirmation message appears; press the pad again to confirm. Deleted Sets cannot be restored. For synced Sets, you must first confirm removal from Cloud before deleting the local copy.

# Using Instruments and Effects

In Note Mode, press a track button to access that track's devices in the Device View. Each track is preloaded with a random Track Preset containing one instrument plus two audio effects.

## Track Presets

Each Track Preset includes an instrument plus two audio effects. Available instruments: Drift (subtractive synthesizer), Drum Rack (16 pads, each with Drum Sampler, one audio effect send, and one audio effect for the entire Rack), Melodic Sampler (repitches a sample across pads), Wavetable (wavetable-based oscillators). Available effects: Auto Filter, Channel EQ, Chorus-Ensemble, Delay, Dynamics, Phaser-Flanger, Redux, Reverb, Saturator. The encoders are mapped to essential sound-shaping parameters that vary between instruments and effects. Drum Sampler has two parameter banks: Main Bank and Filter & Setup. Melodic Sampler has two banks: Main Bank and Setup.

## Browsing and Swapping Presets

Access the browser from the Device View by selecting a Track Preset or individual device with the wheel, then pressing the wheel. Presets are automatically loaded when browsing (Autoload). Navigate categories using the wheel and Back button. Helpful icons: arrow (folder/subfolder navigation), instrument icon (type), effect icon (category), sample icon (sample folder). LED indicators: all pads pulse for Track Presets, all encoders pulse for effects, corresponding pad pulses for Drum Rack samples.

### Browser Categories

Templates: Starting points for creating sounds from scratch. Includes Analog (Drift), FM and WT (Wavetable), Sampler (empty Melodic Sampler), and Drum Rack templates (Drum Kit for one-shots, Choke Kit with choke group, Gate Kit with gate envelope mode). User Presets: Presets created/saved on Move or uploaded via Move Manager. User Recordings: Audio files recorded on Move. User Samples: Samples uploaded via Move Manager.

### Autoload

Autoload is enabled by default. Presets are automatically loaded when browsing. Use Undo to return to previous device. Toggle Autoload off via Workflow Settings (Shift + Step 3). When off, press the wheel to load selected presets.

## Saving Presets

Select the preset in the Device View, press the wheel to enter the browser, hold Shift to access Options submenu, select Save Preset. Saved to User Presets folder. User presets are automatically named after the device but can be renamed in Move Manager.

# Note Mode

Build up a Set by playing and sequencing notes, recording and capturing notes, editing notes and clips. Enter Note Mode from another mode by pressing any track button, or press the Note/Session toggle from Session Mode.

# Playing and Sequencing Notes

For Drums Track Presets, use the 16 pads on the left to play or sequence individual samples. Enable 16 Pitches layout for melodic playing on the right 16 pads. For melodic instruments, all 32 pads can be used in In-Key or Chromatic Mode.

## Pad Layouts, Keys, and Scales

Two modes: In-Key (only notes within selected scale) and Chromatic (all notes). Default: In-Key, C Major, arranged by octaves. Access Keys & Scales menu: hold Shift, press Step 9. In-Key Mode Octaves layout: each row is an octave, root note in track color, others in light gray. Scales with more than eight notes are truncated. In-Key Mode 4ths layout: each pad above another is a perfect fourth higher, each pad to the right is the next scale degree. Chromatic Mode: guitar fretboard arrangement, pad above is five semitones higher (perfect fourth), pad to right is one semitone higher. Root notes in track color, scale notes in light gray, non-scale notes unlit.

## 16 Pitches Layout

For Drums Track Presets, hold Shift and press Step 8 to enable. Select a Drum Rack sample, then play the 16 right pads in various pitches. Available in In-Key or Chromatic mode. Sample pitch cannot be detected; all samples are transposed as if pitch is C. Use plus/minus buttons to change octave range. Notes can be played, sequenced, recorded, and captured.

## Full Velocity

Move's pads are velocity-sensitive by default. Toggle full velocity (value 127) on/off by holding Shift and pressing Step 10.

## Expressive Playing with Polyphonic Aftertouch

All pads are independently pressure-sensitive (Polyphonic Aftertouch). Drift and Wavetable support this type of aftertouch for per-note modulation when holding chords. Move does not support Channel Aftertouch.

## Sequencing Notes

Step buttons sequence notes. Default step grid: 1/16 (each step = sixteenth note). Lines under Step 1, 5, 9, 13 LEDs indicate beat starts. Navigate between bars with left/right arrow buttons. Press a pad then a step button to insert a note. Transport begins immediately when first note is added to empty clip. Step LED colors: white = contains note(s), dim track color = empty step in bar, dim gray = empty clip or outside loop, green = current play position. Multiple notes can be added to same step by pressing several pads simultaneously. By default, step sequencing creates a new clip in the track's first empty clip slot.

# Tempo, Groove, and Metronome Settings

Adjust tempo and groove for a Set, and play or record with a metronome.

## Tempo

Hold Shift and press Step 5 to access. Turn the wheel to adjust. Hold Shift while turning for fine increments. Press the wheel to confirm. Capturing a clip while transport is stopped automatically sets tempo based on playing.

## Groove

Hold Shift and press Step 7. Applies a triplet 16th swing groove to the entire Set. Use the wheel to adjust amount. 0% = no groove, 100% = perfect triplet 16th swing, >100% = exaggerated timing. Hold Shift for fine increments. Groove icon appears beneath Step 7 when active.

## Metronome

Hold Shift and press Step 6 to toggle on/off.

# Editing Notes and Steps

Different note properties (velocity, start position, pitch, length) can be adjusted in Note Mode. Hold multiple steps to adjust properties for several notes simultaneously.

## Velocity

Hold down the corresponding step button, then turn the Volume encoder.

## Note Transposition

For melodic instruments, hold the step button and press plus/minus to raise/lower pitch by a semitone. Long press plus/minus to transpose by an octave. Hold multiple steps to transpose several notes.

## Note Length

Hold the step button and turn the wheel. Right to lengthen, left to shorten. Each click changes length by 10% of a single step's duration. Step LEDs brighten as note extends. Lengths cannot extend beyond the next step that contains a note for Drum Rack or same-note melodic steps.

## Note Nudge

Hold the step and press left/right arrow buttons. Each press nudges by 10% of a step's duration. Hold Shift for 1% nudge. Long press arrow button to nudge by a full step.

## Adjusting Notes in Loop Mode

Press the Loop button to enter Loop Mode. Each bar displayed on step buttons. Hold a step button for a bar and: turn wheel for note lengths, turn Volume encoder for velocities, plus/minus for pitch transposition, left/right arrows for nudging.

## Arpeggiator and Repeat

Hold Shift and press Step 11 to access. Repeat works with both Drum Racks and melodic instruments. Arpeggiator works only with melodic instruments. For Drum Racks: turn wheel to change repeat rate. For melodic instruments: select Repeat Style (Repeat, Arp Up, Arp Down, Arp Random) and adjust Rate. Repeated notes are added to step buttons when recorded or captured.

## Quantizing Notes

Hold Shift and press Step 16. Notes shift according to quantization amount set in Workflow Settings.

## Copying Notes and Step Ranges

Hold Copy button, press source step, release Copy, press destination step. For ranges: hold Copy, press and hold start step, press end step, release Copy and held step, press destination step. Notes can be copied between tracks, clips, and Sets, and between Note Mode steps and Loop Mode bars.

## Adding or Removing Multiple Notes from Steps

For melodic instruments, hold a step to see corresponding notes on pads (white LEDs). Press pads while holding step to add/remove notes. Useful for adjusting chords. Press the step button to remove all notes. In Loop Mode, hold a step to select a bar and press a pad to add that note to all steps in the bar.

# Editing Clips

In Note Mode, you can set loop length, double the loop, duplicate clips, or delete clips.

## Loop Length

Loop length shown beneath Track Preset name. Each line represents a bar: thick line = selected and in loop, thin line = in loop but not selected, plus icon = outside loop. Use left/right arrow buttons to navigate bars. Navigate beyond existing bars to add extra bars. Press Loop button for Loop Mode. In Loop Mode: step LEDs show white (selected), track color (in clip), dim (outside loop). Set loop by pressing start and end steps simultaneously, or press a step twice for one-bar loop. Hold Loop button and turn wheel to adjust length; hold Shift for fine increments.

## Doubling the Loop

Hold Shift and press Step 15 to double the loop including notes and automation.

## Duplicating Clips

Press the Copy button to duplicate the currently selected clip. The duplicated clip becomes the currently selected clip.

## Deleting Clips and Notes

Press Delete to delete the currently playing clip. Hold Delete and press a Drum Rack pad to delete all notes for that pad. In Loop Mode, press Delete for current clip, or hold Delete and press a step to delete all notes in that bar. Press step buttons to remove individual notes.

# Workflow Settings

Hold Shift and press Step 3 to access. Contains quantization amount, step grid resolution, count-in, and Autoload settings.

## Quantize

Press the wheel to select the Quantize entry, then turn the wheel to adjust the quantization amount applied when notes are quantized.

## Step Grid

Select the Step Grid entry to set step grid resolution. Default: 1/16 (bar = 16 steps). Resolutions above 16 divide the bar across multiple pages navigable with arrow buttons. Triplet resolutions available (e.g., 1/16t = 24 steps across two pages with every fourth step deactivated).

## Count-In and Autoload

Count-In is enabled by default for recording. Toggle via Count-In On entry. Autoload is enabled by default for immediate preset preview when browsing. Toggle via Autoload entry; when off, press wheel to load selected presets.

# Recording and Capturing

Record notes from pads or external MIDI instruments into new or existing clips. Capture notes without recording. Sequencing via step buttons also available. Recording is only possible in Note Mode.

## Recording Notes

Tracks are automatically armed in Note Mode. Press the Record button to start recording (after count-in if enabled). Record button: solid red = recording, dimly lit = ready, unlit = disabled. Stop recording by pressing Record, switching tracks, or pressing Play. When recording into an empty clip slot, clip length extends until you stop. If you stop during the first half of the last bar, that bar is excluded. Create fixed-length clips by setting loop length in Loop Mode before recording.

### Recording with Count-In and Metronome

Count-in plays metronome for one bar before recording starts. Record button flashes during count-in for new clips, stays red for overdubs. Toggle count-in in Workflow Settings (Shift + Step 3). Toggle metronome with Shift + Step 6.

## Recording Automation

Record sound changes from device/effect parameter adjustments during playback. Select the device in Device View, press Record, adjust encoders. Filled circle indicates recorded automation, encoder LED turns red. Can only automate one device at a time but can switch between devices while recording. Stop by pressing Record, Play, or switching tracks.

### Deactivating Automation

Hold Mute button and tap the encoder to deactivate/reactivate. Empty circle indicates inactive automation. You can temporarily override recorded automation by adjusting an encoder during playback; release to resume recorded automation.

### Checking Automation Status

Hold the Mute button and check encoder LEDs: unlit = no automation, red = active automation, white = deactivated automation.

### Deleting Automation

Hold the Delete button and tap the respective encoder.

### Per-Step Automation

Select the device in Device View, hold a step button containing a note, turn an encoder. Step LEDs following the held step turn red indicating the automation time range (typically extends to next note). Multiple steps can have per-step automation added simultaneously.

## Capturing Notes and Automation

Tracks are automatically armed, so you can capture without pressing Record. Press the Capture button after playing. If transport isn't running, Move detects tempo and generates three BPM estimates. Turn wheel to choose different suggested tempo. Selecting a different estimate may change loop length but not playback speed. Automation can be captured by adjusting encoders and pressing Capture. Overdub by playing/adjusting while existing clip is in focus. Hold Shift and press Capture to clear input without capturing.

# Sampling

Use Sampling Mode to sample audio from Move's built-in microphone, audio input, USB-C, or to resample its main output. Audio is recorded and saved to a pad in a single interaction. Sample into Drum Rack pads or melodic pads.

## Sampling Mode

Press the Sampling button to enter Sampling Mode. Sampling button LED pulses red. Display shows 'Press pad' prompt. Drum Rack pad LEDs turn pink for drum-based presets. Root note pad LEDs turn pink for melodic/synth presets. Sampling Mode closes automatically when finished recording or when pressing Sampling button, Back button, or switching tracks/modes.

## Selecting a Sampling Input Source

Four input sources: Mic, Line in (when audio source connected), Resampling (main output), USB-C. One source at a time. Touch wheel to view current source. Press wheel to switch source. Icons: microphone (Mic), rectangle with arrow (Line in), speaker (Resampling), USB-C cable (USB-C). Last selected source is remembered.

## Recording Samples

Press Sampling button, then press a pad to start recording. Pad LED pulses red. Press same pad, any other pad, or Sampling button to stop. You can hold a pad to record and release to stop. Maximum recording length: 240 seconds (4 minutes). When sampling into a melodic track with a synthesizer preset, the instrument swaps to Melodic Sampler. Recorded samples appear in browser under User Recordings. Samples are named based on Set name and recording number.

## Mic and Line In Sampling

Additional options for adjusting input gain and turning monitoring on/off. Gain: turn wheel to select input levels icon, press wheel to open Gain option, turn wheel to adjust. Monitoring: headphones icon, press wheel to toggle. Default: Monitoring Off for audio output connections, Monitoring On for audio input connections. Monitoring remains active after exiting Sampling Mode if audio device is connected.

## Resampling

When Resampling is selected, you can sample Move's overall output. If transport isn't running, playback starts when you press a pad, syncing the sample start with playback start.

## Sampling Audio via USB-C

Sample audio from a connected computer, smartphone, or tablet via USB-C. Monitoring toggle available (default: Off). Devices with USB-C ports work with USB-C cable alone. Lightning port devices require an adapter and charger. Sampling via USB-C works only with USB Audio Class 2.0 protocol; some Android devices may not be compatible.

## Multi-pad Recording

Record the same sample onto separate Drum Rack pads with different sample start times. Select a Drum Rack track, activate Sampling Mode, press a pad to start, then press different pads to continue recording onto each. Previous pad LEDs change from pink to track color. Stop by pressing current pad again or Sampling button.

## Sample Slicing

Non-destructively divide a sample into slices assigned to individual Drum Rack pads. Enter Slicing Mode: hold Shift and press the wheel while a sample is in focus, then select Slice. Turn wheel to change number of slices. Use first two encoders for sample region start/end points. Third encoder adjusts individual slice start points. Press wheel or Back to confirm. Creates new Drum Rack with slices. Press Undo to restore original Drum Rack.

## Adjusting Sample Parameters

Use encoders to adjust sample parameters. Hold a pad and turn Volume encoder for sample gain. Drum Sampler has Main Bank and Filter & Setup banks. Melodic Sampler has Main Bank and Setup banks. Switch banks: highlight instrument/sample with wheel, hold Shift, press and turn wheel.

### Sample Parameters in Drum Sampler

Main Bank parameters (left to right): 1. Transpose/Detune — semitones (hold Shift for cents). 2. Sample Start — starting point (hold Shift to zoom waveform). 3. Attack — time to reach peak level. 4. Hold — duration at peak level. 5. Decay/Length — Decay: fade to silence time; hold Shift+tap for Length parameter. 6. Playback Effect — Stretch, Loop, Pitch Env, Punch, 8-Bit, FM, Ring Mod, Sub Osc, or Noise. Each has two unique parameters on encoders 7 and 8. Filter & Setup bank parameters: 1. Filter Frequency — cutoff point. 2. Filter Resonance — emphasis near cutoff. 3. Filter Type — Low-Pass 12, Low-Pass 24, High-Pass 24, Peak. 4. Envelope Mode — Trigger (plays for Hold duration) or Gate (plays while pad held). 5. Choke — on/off choke group (one choke group per Drum Rack). 6. Velocity > … — target parameter modulated by velocity: Filter, Attack, Hold, Decay, FX 1, FX 2. 7. Velocity > (target) or Velocity > Vol — modulation amount for target; hold Shift+tap for Velocity > Vol. 8. Pan — stereo position.

### Sample Parameters in the Melodic Sampler

Main Bank parameters (left to right): 1. Transpose/Detune — semitones (hold Shift for cents). 2. Sample Start — starting point (hold Shift to zoom). 3. Attack — time to peak level. 4. Decay — fade to silence from peak. 5. Release/Playback Length — Release: silence after pad release; hold Shift+tap for Playback Length. 6. Filter Frequency — cutoff point. 7. Filter Resonance — emphasis near cutoff. 8. Filter LFO/LFO Rate — Filter LFO: LFO modulation of filter cutoff; hold Shift+tap for LFO Rate (Hz). Setup bank parameters: 1. Filter Type — Low-Pass, Band-Pass, High-Pass. 2. Envelope Mode — Trigger (plays for Decay duration) or Gate (plays while pad held).

# Mixing

The Volume encoder adjusts volume of individual tracks, pads, Sets, and overall output during playback.

## Output Volume

Turn the Volume encoder while in Set Overview, Note Mode, or Session Mode. VU meter on display indicates current level.

## Track Volume

Hold the corresponding track button and turn the Volume encoder. Track volume in dB shown on display. Track button LEDs indicate volume levels during playback: track color for low/medium, white for high.

## Soloing Tracks

In Note or Session Mode, hold Shift and Mute buttons, then press a track button. Soloed track LED turns blue. Same steps to unsolo.

## Muting Tracks

Hold Mute button, press a track button. Muted track button LED dims. In Note Mode, press Mute alone to mute selected track. Hold Mute and press track button to unmute.

## Muting Drum Rack Pads

Hold Mute button and press the Drum Rack pad. Muted pad LED changes to dimly lit white. Hold Mute and press again to unmute.

## Sample Gain

In Note Mode, hold the pad and turn the Volume encoder. Display shows waveform expanding/contracting with a white line indicating gain level.

## Set Volume

In Set Overview, hold the Set's pad and turn the Volume encoder. All four track volumes adjusted simultaneously. VU meter shows overall output.

## Built-in Limiter

Each Set includes a hidden, non-adjustable built-in Limiter to prevent clipping and distortion. When opening a Move Set in Live, the Limiter appears on Live's Main track.

# Session Mode

Session Mode lets you view and manage all clips in a Set, adjust main effects, arrange clips into scenes, and launch clips during performance. Each row of pads represents a track's clip slots (up to 8 clips per track) and each column represents a scene (up to 4 clips per scene). Pad LED states: unlit = empty slot, track color = existing clip, white = selected empty slot, pulsing white = selected existing clip, pulsing track color = clip about to stop, pulsing green = clip queued for playback.

## Working with Clips

Creating: select empty slot, switch to Note Mode, record/capture/sequence notes. Launching: press a clip's pad for immediate playback; if transport running, starts at next bar. Press Play to start all selected clips. Slide finger vertically over a column to play a scene. Copying: hold Copy, press source clip pad, press destination pad. Deleting: hold Delete, press clip pad. Retriggering: hold Shift and press Play to restart all playing clips from beginning (works in Note Mode too).

## Adjusting a Set's Main Effects

Each Set includes two adjustable audio effects (default: Dynamics and Saturator). Available effects: Auto Filter, Channel EQ, Chorus-Ensemble, Delay, Dynamics, Phaser-Flanger, Redux, Reverb, Saturator. Select effect with wheel, adjust parameters with encoders in Session Mode.

# Control Live Mode

Move can function as a dedicated control surface for Live. Connect via USB-C, open Live, then switch via Setup menu (Shift + Step 2, select Control Live). Live automatically detects Move and shows the Session Ring in Session View. Control Live Mode is supported with Live 12 version 12.1+ and Live 11 version 11.3.35+. Move can also be used as an audio interface and access Move Manager in this mode.

## Using Move's Controls in Control Live Mode

Modifier buttons: Shift (fine adjustments), Mute (mute tracks/pads), Delete (delete notes/clips/scenes/tracks), Copy (duplicate). Transport: Play (start/stop, Shift+Play for retrigger), Record (start/stop recording). Functions: Capture (capture MIDI, no automation capture), Undo (undo/redo with Shift), Back, Note/Session toggle. Wheel: navigate devices, press to fold/unfold Racks, hold Mute+press to deactivate device. Volume encoder: Main track volume by default, hold track button for track volume. Encoders adjust selected device parameters. Shift+Step shortcuts: Step 3 (Workflow Settings), Step 5 (Tempo), Step 6 (Metronome), Step 7 (Groove/Global Groove Amount), Step 9 (Key and Scale), Step 10 (Full Velocity), Step 14 (prepare next clip slot). Features NOT available in Control Live Mode: 16 Pitches layout, Arpeggiator, Capturing automation, Sampling Mode.

## Selecting Tracks in Control Live Mode

Note Mode: select tracks via track buttons (four at a time). Select additional tracks in Live to bring them into focus. Session Mode: press odd-numbered step buttons to select tracks including return tracks and Main track. First seven tracks in columns, last column is Main track. Use left/right arrows to navigate. White LED = selected, track color LED = not selected, red LED = armed. Track operations: hold track button + Volume encoder (volume), Mute + track button (mute/unmute), Delete + track button (delete), Copy + track button (duplicate), Record + track button or double-press (arm/unarm).

## Note Mode Features in Control Live Mode

Three sub-modes: Instrument, Drum, Slicing (determined by device type). Common controls across sub-modes: Mute+track button (mute/unmute), Delete (delete clip, hold+pad for notes/samples/slices), Copy (duplicate clip, hold for note copy), Loop button (Loop Mode with wheel/Shift adjustments), left/right arrows (navigate bars, Shift for 16-bar increments). Shift+Step shortcuts: Step 11 (repeat rate), Step 15 (double loop), Step 16 (quantize). Step sequencing works in all sub-modes; press pad then step to add notes.

### Instrument Sub-Mode

Used for any instrument except Drum Rack or Simpler in Slicing Playback Mode. Play, sequence, record, or capture in In-Key or Chromatic Mode. Plus/minus buttons shift octave range. Hold Shift+plus/minus for one scale degree. Hold step+plus/minus to transpose by semitone, long press for octave.

### Drum Sub-Mode

Used when track contains a Drum Rack. All 32 pads available. Select pad: hold Shift+press pad. Hold pad+Volume encoder for chain volume. Hold Mute+press pad for mute/unmute. Hold Copy+press source pad then destination pad for device copy. Plus/minus navigate within Drum Rack (16 pads at a time, hold Shift for 4 pads).

### Slicing Sub-Mode

Used when track contains Simpler in Slicing Playback Mode. Play, sequence, record, or capture slices. Hold Shift+press pad to select a slice. Plus/minus navigate between pages of 16 slices (hold Shift for 4 slices).

## Session Mode Features in Control Live Mode

Two sub-modes: Default and Session Overview. Switch with Shift+Note/Session toggle. In both: odd step buttons select tracks, even step buttons stop playing clips. Step 15 = Main track, Step 16 = stop all clips. Left/right arrows move Session Ring horizontally (Shift for 7 tracks). Plus/minus move vertically (Shift for 4 scenes).

### Default Sub-Mode

Vertical columns = tracks, horizontal rows = scenes. Press pads in track columns to launch clips. Eighth column = Main track (green LEDs) for launching scenes. Hold Shift+press pad to select clip/scene. Hold Delete+press pad to delete clip/scene. Hold Copy for clip copy or scene duplication.

### Session Overview Sub-Mode

Each pad represents a block of four clips across seven tracks. Press a pad to move Session Ring to that section. Dim LED = block with clips, white LED = selected, pulsing green = unselected block with playing clips.

# USB Operation Modes

Move's USB connections carry data and power, functioning differently in Standalone vs Control Live Mode.

## USB Connections in Standalone Mode

USB-C port: charge via power supply, connect to computer for audio interface or Move Manager access. USB-A port: connect MIDI devices. Move Manager access via Wi-Fi (same network) using hostname and authentication code. Computer connection charges Move if computer supplies at least 7.5W.

## USB Connections in Control Live Mode

USB-C connects to computer for control surface functionality (software version 1.1+). Move can be used as audio interface (input only to computer). Move Manager accessible for file transfers. USB-A MIDI port not available in this mode. Charges if computer provides at least 7.5W.

# Move Control Reference

Set: Each piece of music, containing four tracks, up to 32 per Move, auto-saved. Track: Each Set has four tracks with random Track Presets, switched via track buttons. Track Preset: One instrument and two effects per preset. Clip: Container for notes (up to 8 per track), added via sequencing/recording/capturing. Set Overview: Contains all Sets; create, copy, delete, upload/remove from Cloud, change pad colors. Note Mode: Build Sets by recording, capturing, editing clips, playing and sequencing notes, adjusting presets. Session Mode: Arrange, copy, launch clips; adjust global effects; perform. Wheel: Navigate/select menu entries, confirm selections. Play: Start/stop playback; Shift+Play retriggers active clips in Session Mode. Record: Start/stop recording; records notes and automation; overdub into existing clips. Back: Exit menus/browser/Sampling Mode; navigate back in browser/menus; hold to exit current view. Note/Session Toggle: Switch between Note and Session Mode; hold to preview other mode. Track Buttons: Select tracks in Note/Session Mode; temporarily hold to view without selecting. Encoders: Adjust device parameters; Volume encoder for tracks/pads/Sets/output; touching shows parameter on display. Pads: 32 velocity-sensitive silicone pads with Polyphonic Aftertouch (Drift and Wavetable). Step Buttons: Sequence notes (1/16 default); beat markers under Steps 1, 5, 9, 13; in Loop Mode represent bars; hold Shift for feature access icons. Capture: Capture played notes/automation without recording; tempo detection when transport stopped. Sampling: Enter Sampling Mode for mic/input/resampling/USB-C audio; record to pads. Loop: Enter Loop Mode showing bars on step buttons; duplicate bars, adjust loop length, edit all notes. Mute: Mute/unmute tracks or Drum Rack pads. Delete: Delete Sets/clips/notes/automation. Copy: Copy Sets/notes/step ranges/bars/clips; press again to cancel. Undo: Undo last action; Shift+Undo to redo. Plus/Minus: Change octave range or transpose pitches. Left/Right Arrows: Navigate between bars or nudge notes.
