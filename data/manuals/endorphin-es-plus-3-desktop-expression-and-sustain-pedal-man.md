# Introduction

PLUS 3 is a desktop expression and sustain pedal that adds controls and unlocks features in MIDI controllers, synthesizers, effects pedals, and semi-modular/eurorack instruments — some in ways previously not possible.

Designed for musicians who prefer compact desktop control of features accessible via the sustain and expression jacks of their instruments, as opposed to using feet or bulky sustain and expression pedals on a desk.

# Overview

PLUS 3 includes a high-quality fader for expressive performance and automation, a momentary sustain key, and an innovative 'drone mode' toggle switch which lets you free your hands and hold notes indefinitely. In drone mode, the momentary key's functionality changes to let you swap droned notes with new ones.

Comes bundled with three cables: a 1-meter stereo/balanced TRS cable for the expression side, a 1-meter mono/instrument TS cable for the sustain side, and a TRS Y-breakout cable for connecting to semi-modular and Eurorack gear. The breakout cable lets you use the expression side as an attenuator and the sustain side as a gate control for CV.

Fully passive (no power required). Made of aluminum — sturdy, light, and portable. The sustain key uses standard mechanical keyboard-style switches and can be customized/swapped.

Features an on-panel TRS/RTS switch for various expression jack configurations, and an internal 'hidden' jumper that lets you use the sustain side as an expression toggle.

# Interface

The PLUS 3 has six main interface elements described below.

## Expression Slider

A 50mm 10kΩ sliding potentiometer/fader to control expression. Used with the dedicated TRS-TRS cable plugged into the Expression Jack.

## Expression Jack

A 1/4" TRS connector controlled by the Expression Slider. Connect to the 'expression' control of a synthesizer or pedal. Alternatively, use the breakout cable to connect to modular instruments and use the Expression Slider as an attenuator.

## Expression TRS/RTS Switch

A two-position polarity selector for expression:
- UPPER/TOP position: TRS type (typical Roland EV-5 or Moog EP-3 configuration)
- LOWER/BOTTOM position: RTS type

Using the wrong polarity will yield nonlinear control or other unexpected results.

## Momentary Sustain Button

A momentary key switch to control sustain. Used with the dedicated TS-TS cable connected to the Sustain Jack.

## Reverse/Latch/Sustain Toggle Switch

Defines whether the action of the Momentary Sustain Button is reversed. Toggles between an 'always on' state (where pressing the sustain button momentarily changes to 'off') or vice versa. This enables drone mode.

## Sustain Jack

A 1/4" TS connector controlled by the combined states of the Momentary Sustain Button and the Sustain Toggle Switch. Connect to the 'sustain' or 'footswitch' control of a synthesizer or pedal. Can also connect to modular gear using the breakout cable (set internal jumper to TRS or RTS mode).

# Sustain Side

Capabilities unlocked for instruments with a sustain jack:
- Momentary sustain button for instruments without a dedicated physical sustain control
- Dedicated drone/hold switch for instruments that lack one
- 'UNSUSTAIN' mode: swap held/drone notes by playing new notes and tapping the momentary sustain button to release previously held notes
- Even instruments with a hold button may gain additional functionality via the sustain jack (e.g., piano VSTs resonating sympathetic strings)
- Hands-on arpeggiator latch for synths without an arp hold button
- Momentary and toggle control of any parameter mappable in DAW synth mod matrix/menu system or effect pedal (sostenuto, freeze effect, dry/wet bypass, swapping presets, etc.)
- Sostenuto support: drones notes held while engaging sustain, lets you play un-held notes on top
- Enhanced arpeggiator riffs: holding sustain while a pattern arpeggiates can dramatically enhance its sound (most arps can't tie notes beyond 100%)
- CV or audio mute with Eurorack gear (set the hidden jumper to TRS or RTS mode)

# Using Both PLUS 3 and a Sustain Footswitch Pedal

You can use both a regular sustain pedal and PLUS 3 simultaneously with a TS splitter accessory.

Requirements and tips:
1. Use a TS splitter (TS male 1/4" mono splitting to two TS female 1/4" jacks). Do NOT use a TRS breakout/Y/insert/ring-tip or stereo to left/right cable.
2. Connect PLUS 3 and your sustain pedal to the TS splitter, plug the splitter into your instrument's sustain jack.
3. If both your instrument/MIDI controller and sustain pedal allow polarity selection (options may be labeled -/+, 'invert', 'normally open'/'normally closed', 'KORG'/'other'), try all four combinations (--, -+, +-, ++) until one works.
4. Recommended budget sustain pedals with polarity switches: M-Audio SP-2, Alesis ASP-1 MKII. Half-damper sustain pedals might not work.
5. Some instruments auto-detect polarity on plug-in or power-up. In that case, plug everything in first, then power on the controller. Experiment with PLUS 3's Sustain Toggle Switch and pedal polarity switch combinations.
6. In this configuration, the sustain pedal works only as a regular sustain pedal. PLUS 3's drone mode unsustain still requires the PLUS 3 Momentary Button.

# Expression Side

Capabilities unlocked for instruments with an expression jack:
- Adds an expressive physical fader to MIDI controllers that don't have faders but have an expression jack (e.g., Native Instruments Kontrol MK3, M32; Korg KeyStage)
- Direct access and expressive control of any single parameter mappable in DAW, effects pedal, or synth (bowing a violin, orchestral swell, fine tuning a track's mix level)
- Control the last parameter edited in DAW: choose a parameter with mouse, edit with fader (works in Ableton Live Suite via free Max for Live 'kozel'; in Cubase, use sustain button to lock a parameter and fader to control it)
- Macros: use the fader as a macro control to change multiple parameters at once for DAWs, synths with mod matrices, or effect pedals with expression jack mapping (Chase Bliss, Boss, EHX pedals, Endorphin.es GHOST and GOLDEN MASTER pedals)
- Map to numerous destinations for sound shaping (build-ups, drops)
- If your synth has a bouncy joystick instead of a mod wheel (some Korg and Roland synths), map the fader to control mod wheel (typically CC#01 or Joystick Y) for non-bouncy mod control

# TRS/RTS Types for Expression

All expression pedals work on the same principle: a variable resistor (slider) inside. A reference voltage is sent from the keyboard, MIDI controller, or guitar pedal, and the slider position determines the voltage returned.

## TRS Mode

In most cases the reference voltage comes into the RING pin of the 1/4" connector, gets divided/attenuated via the slider from maximum to zero, and returns via the TIP. Set the Expression TRS/RTS Switch to UPPER/TOP position for TRS type.

## RTS Mode

Some instruments send voltage to the TIP pin and expect it back from the RING pin. Set the Expression TRS/RTS Switch to LOWER/BOTTOM position for RTS type.

Switching to RTS mode also helps when using the expression part as attenuator with Y-cables and stereo jacks on reception. In TRS mode, the ring may be shorted to sleeve (ground) causing hum when touching the body. Switching to RTS fixes this.

PLUS 3 has a protection resistor to prevent damage from wrong polarity choice. However, the TRS/RTS switch will change the attenuation curve — swap position if you're not getting a linear response.

## TS Mode

Some companies like Line 6 use TIP-SLEEVE (TS) type expression pedals, meaning their devices only use two jack pins instead of three. PLUS 3 is compatible with TS expression: use the included 1/4" TS-TS cable (or any mono cable) for the expression part. Set the TRS/RTS switch to UPPER/TRS position.

Note: When using the included TRS to left/right TS adapter, the red connector corresponds to TIP/left channel and black corresponds to RING/right channel.

# The Hidden Jumper

PLUS 3's sustain side has a hidden jumper-based selector inside the enclosure (labeled 'SUSTAIN AS EXPR.'). By default, the sustain side responds to TS-type cables (also shorting TIP with RING while using TRS cable). The default jumper position is floating on the middle pin.

To use the sustain side as a TOGGLE EXPRESSION for TRS/RTS-style cables and jacks, swap the jumper position (otherwise you'll only get part of the useful expression range).

With the jumper installed on a pair of pins, the sustain side behaves exactly as the expression side — just in a momentary way, inverting depending on the latch switch.

Jumper positions:
- Middle pin to right pin: TRS type devices
- Middle pin to left pin: RTS type devices

What the jumper does: shunts (pulls down) the TIP (for TRS) or RING (for RTS) with a 10kΩ resistor to ground (sleeve), preventing floating expression input when the switch is OFF and enabling full expression range.

Disassembly instructions: Remove the fader cap, unscrew the top screw and two side screws using a PH1 Phillips screwdriver. Do NOT remove the nuts around the expression and sustain jacks. To reassemble, use the fader shaft to align with the top panel screw hole, screw in without over-tightening, replace fader cap.

# Troubleshooting

**Fader not reaching maximum value (e.g., CC value of 90 instead of 127) or reaching max far from end of fader travel:**
Some instruments require calibration of the bottom and top fader positions (e.g., NI KONTROL and KOMPLETE keyboards).

**Fader control isn't linear:**
- Flip the TRS/RTS switch to the other position
- Check the expression curve settings in your instrument — it may not be set to linear

**Ground loop noise:**
- On expression side: swap positions on the RTS/TRS switch
- On sustain side: check the Hidden Jumper position — make sure it's on TRS or RTS

**Clicks when using sustain side to mute Eurorack audio:**
The sustain side is designed for fast response, which is great for gating CV but may cause clicks on audio. Pressing the button down slowly may help due to the two-stage circuit design (similar to two-way light switching).

**PLUS 3 connected but not working:**
- Ensure correct cable for each jack: Expression side uses TRS cable (two black rings), Sustain side uses TS cable (one black ring)
- Ensure correct jack connection: Sustain side connects to jacks labeled 'sustain' or 'footswitch'; Expression side connects to jacks labeled 'expression' or 'control'
- Check synth/pedal/controller settings for dual-purpose jacks that support both sustain and expression

**Synth/MIDI controller only has a sustain jack:**
- Some controllers (Arturia Minilab 3, Native Instruments M32, A25) let you choose jack behavior — sustain or expression
- You can connect one side of PLUS 3 to one instrument and the other side to a different instrument

**Incompatible instruments for expression fader:**
PLUS 3 uses a 10kΩ expression sliding potentiometer for broad compatibility. Some instruments require higher resistance pots (50kΩ or 100kΩ). Higher-resistance pedals like the Moog EV-3 may work better for those instruments, but note that high-resistance pedals won't reach maximum range on devices expecting 10kΩ.
